# The “Slice and Dice” Approach to Movement

## Definition
This concept describes a method of breaking down complex movement concepts into smaller, more manageable “slices” and “dices” – a process of deliberate deconstruction and exploration.

## Explanation
Instead of relying on rigid definitions and categories, this approach encourages experimentation and a willingness to discard preconceived notions. It’s about creating a dynamic, evolving understanding of movement.

## Key Insight
A flexible, iterative approach to learning movement is more effective than fixed definitions.

## Examples
*   Writing down movement ideas, then crumpling and discarding them to avoid getting stuck in a particular framework.
