# 2. The Illusion of Fixed Identity

## Definition
We tend to construct a fixed sense of self – a “me” – based on memories, past experiences, and projected futures. This fixed identity creates illusions and unnecessary suffering, preventing us from fully engaging with the present moment.

## Explanation
This section highlights the human tendency to create a rigid, self-defined identity. This fixed identity, built on past and future projections, limits our ability to truly experience the present and creates a barrier to genuine transformation.

## Key Insight
“The ways in which we lie to ourselves frame our reality.” – Recognizing the self-deceptive nature of our constructed identities is the first step toward liberation.

## Examples
*   The story of Vika’s upbringing, where she was raised to strive for “excellence” – a fixed ideal that ultimately led to emptiness.
*   The example of the partner, who was “self-delusional” and impacted Vika’s perception of reality.
