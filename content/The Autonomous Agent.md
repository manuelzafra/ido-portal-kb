# The Autonomous Agent

## Definition
A state of being characterized by independent learning, self-directed development, and freedom from rigid definitions or external constraints.

## Explanation
This describes a person who has cultivated the ability to learn and grow without being limited by pre-defined roles, expectations, or cultural narratives. It’s about embracing uncertainty and forging one’s own path.

## Key Insight
True development comes from cultivating autonomy – the ability to learn and evolve independently.

## Examples
The speaker’s desire to remain “free from much more deeper definitions” and to pursue a practice guided by an undefined “higher thing.”

## Relationships
* Related to: [[The Practice of Unknowing]], [[The Intention-Driven Practice]]
* Opposes: [[Collector Mentality]] (being defined by external trends or systems)

## Notes
This concept represents a desired state of being – a powerful agent capable of continuous growth and adaptation.
