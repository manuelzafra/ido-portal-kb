# 3. Critical Mass in Movement

## Definition
A threshold of experience and understanding in movement that, once reached, triggers a profound and irreversible transformation, leading to exponential growth and capability.

## Explanation
This concept describes a point of no return where accumulated knowledge and practice coalesce into a fundamental shift in movement ability and awareness. It’s not simply about adding more training hours, but about achieving a critical level of understanding that unlocks new potential.

## Key Insight
“Once that critical mass has been achieved, there is a paradoxical thing about it that you can work for 20 years and go nowhere to go everywhere in a moment.” – Highlights the non-linear nature of movement transformation.

## Examples
The discussion about the CrossFit visual, the emphasis on years of training, and the “mat masters” illustrate this concept.
