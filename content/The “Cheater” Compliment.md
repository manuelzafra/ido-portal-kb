# The “Cheater” Compliment

## Definition
A positive reinforcement signal indicating a successful adaptation to unexpected or challenging movement demands, signifying a high level of movement intelligence and adaptability.

## Explanation
This concept describes a specific form of praise – “that’s the best compliment” – used to acknowledge and validate a learner’s ability to respond effectively to unexpected stimuli. It signifies that the learner has demonstrated a high level of movement intelligence, exhibiting the capacity to adapt, improvise, and overcome challenges.

## Key Insight
Positive feedback, specifically the “cheater” compliment, serves as a powerful signal of movement intelligence and adaptability.
