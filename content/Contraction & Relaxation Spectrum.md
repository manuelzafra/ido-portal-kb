# Contraction & Relaxation Spectrum

## Definition
This concept proposes that all movement fundamentally exists on a spectrum between contraction and relaxation, representing a dynamic interplay of force and ease.

## Explanation
Rather than categorizing movement into distinct types (e.g., strength, speed), it’s more accurate to view movement as a continuous gradient between these two opposing states.

## Key Insight
Movement is fundamentally a balance between tension and release.

## Examples
*   A weightlifter’s explosive contraction followed by a controlled relaxation.
*   A swimmer’s rhythmic undulation, constantly shifting between contraction and relaxation.
