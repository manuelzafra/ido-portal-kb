# 4. Time is a Commodity

## Definition
The understanding that time is a finite and valuable resource, urging a mindful approach to how it is spent and utilized, particularly in movement practice.

## Explanation
Ido’s statement, “we’re all gonna die. And we might think we’re here doing grand things with grand plans, but we’re just passing time until we go into the grave,” frames movement practice not as a means to an end, but as a way to make the most of the time we have.

## Key Insight
Movement practice should be approached with a sense of urgency and intentionality, recognizing the preciousness of time.

## Examples
The conversation about making the most of the time until death underscores the importance of prioritizing meaningful experiences and activities.

## Relationships
* Related to: [[Stoicism]]
* Opposes: [[Procrastination]]

## Notes
This concept encourages a shift in perspective, moving away from extrinsic motivations (e.g., achieving a specific skill) and towards intrinsic value (e.g., experiencing the joy of movement).
