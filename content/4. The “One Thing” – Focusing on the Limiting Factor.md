# 4. The “One Thing” – Focusing on the Limiting Factor

## Definition
This framework suggests identifying and addressing the single most limiting factor in a process or system, rather than attempting to tackle multiple issues simultaneously.

## Explanation
This approach prioritizes focused action, recognizing that complex problems often stem from a single root cause. By identifying and addressing that root cause, the rest of the system will naturally improve.

## Key Insight
“What can I do right now? What’s the limiting factor?”

## Examples
The example of the school expansion – focusing on attracting a flow of students rather than trying to improve teaching quality or school facilities simultaneously.

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Pareto Principle (80/20 Rule)]]
*   Opposes: [[Multitasking]]

## Notes
This is a powerful tool for prioritization and efficiency, particularly in complex systems.
