# 6. The Importance of "Driving" the Body

## Definition
The ability to control and manipulate the body's movement in a skilled and efficient manner, similar to how a driver controls a vehicle.

## Explanation
This concept suggests that movement is not simply a passive process but an active control system that requires focused attention, coordination, and feedback.

## Key Insight
“You give them any car, Toyota, and you go against them with a formula one, and they win.” – This illustrates the importance of developing a fundamental movement skill – a “movement sense” – before attempting more complex techniques.

## Examples
*   Comparing movement to driving: “You give them any car, Toyota, and you go against them with a formula one, and they win.” – This illustrates the importance of developing a fundamental movement skill before attempting more complex techniques.
