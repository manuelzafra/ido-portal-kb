# The Force of Constant Change

## Definition
The inherent tendency of systems – both physical and internal – to evolve and shift, driven by external forces and internal processes, requiring constant adaptation and awareness.

## Explanation
This concept describes the dynamic nature of reality, emphasizing that nothing remains static. It’s rooted in Heraclitus’s idea of “panta rhei” (everything flows), and highlights the importance of recognizing and responding to this constant flux.

## Key Insight
“If you are not transforming, then since everything else is transforming, since time is running forward and all these horses are acting upon you and everything changes around, then you are in collision.” – This underscores the danger of resisting change and the necessity of embracing it as a core element of the practice.

## Examples
*   The body adapting to a new exercise routine through muscle growth and increased flexibility.
*   A business responding to changing market trends by innovating and adjusting its strategy.
