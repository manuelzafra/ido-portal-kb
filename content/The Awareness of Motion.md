# The Awareness of Motion

## Definition
Movement practice begins with a fundamental awareness of the body’s constant state of motion – the movement of the body, mind, and emotions – as a primary object of investigation.

## Explanation
This is the foundational layer of any good movement practice. It’s about recognizing that movement isn’t a static performance but a continuous, flowing process, and that this awareness itself is the core benefit. It’s about paying attention to the subtle shifts and changes that occur within the body as it moves.

## Key Insight
Movement practice fundamentally begins with the awareness of the body’s inherent motion, a state of constant flux.

## Examples
Ido’s observation of his own playfulness during a walk, and his awareness of his body’s sensations and the way things make him feel.
