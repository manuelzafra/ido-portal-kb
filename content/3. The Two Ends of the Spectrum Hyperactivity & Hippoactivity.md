# 3. The Two Ends of the Spectrum: Hyperactivity & Hippoactivity

## Definition
Movement exists on a spectrum between excessive, potentially damaging exertion (hyperactivity) and insufficient movement (hippoactivity), both of which can lead to chronic issues.

## Explanation
This framework highlights the dangers of both extremes – pushing the body beyond its adaptive capacity and neglecting the fundamental need for movement. It’s a reminder that “more” isn’t always “better.”

## Key Insight
The most common problems arise from imbalances – a combination of overuse and underuse.

## Examples
*   A runner who consistently logs 10+ miles daily (hyperactivity) versus someone who sits for 8 hours a day (hippoactivity).
