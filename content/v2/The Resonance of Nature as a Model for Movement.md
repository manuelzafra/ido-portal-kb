# The Resonance of Nature as a Model for Movement

## Definition
This concept posits that authentic movement practices should reflect the organic, dynamic patterns observed in nature and animal behavior, rather than adhering to rigid, linear structures or Westernized ideals.

## Explanation
The speaker contrasts the linearity of modern yoga with the fluid, curvilinear movements found in traditional dances like Thai and Chinese dance, and the natural movements of animals. This isn't merely an aesthetic preference; it suggests a deeper principle: that true movement arises from a responsiveness to internal and external forces, mirroring the adaptability and resilience of natural systems. The "soul rounded, soul curly" quality of these movements signifies a connection to a deeper, more intuitive intelligence than can be achieved through purely intellectual or linear approaches. This aligns with biomimicry, the practice of emulating nature's designs and processes to solve human problems.

## Key Insight
Authentic movement is not about imposing form, but about allowing oneself to be shaped by the inherent intelligence of the body and the patterns of the natural world.

## Examples
* **Text Example:** "Look at Thai dance, look at Chinese dances, martial arts, soul rounded, soul curly, it's like nature, what you see in nature and the movement of the animals."
* **Outside Example:** Observing how a tree bends in the wind, rather than breaking, provides a model for resilience and adaptability in movement and life.
