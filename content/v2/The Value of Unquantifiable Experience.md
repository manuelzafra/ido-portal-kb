# The Value of Unquantifiable Experience

## Definition
The recognition that human experience, particularly in domains like movement and skill acquisition, contains a wealth of information that cannot be captured by numerical data or standardized metrics. This necessitates a shift in focus from quantifiable outcomes to qualitative observation and intuitive understanding.

## Explanation
The discussion around Ido Portal’s philosophy emphasizes the limitations of a purely quantitative approach to movement. While CrossFit utilizes WODs and measurable benchmarks, Portal argues that these metrics often miss the point – the nuanced development of movement intelligence, adaptability, and neurological coordination. The anecdote about the police interrupting the moving conversation highlights the value of spontaneous, unscripted interactions that cannot be planned or quantified.  This concept extends beyond movement; it underscores the importance of embracing the richness of subjective experience in any field.  It challenges the modern obsession with data-driven decision-making, reminding us that some of the most valuable insights emerge from qualitative exploration and intuitive understanding.

## Key Insight
The most profound learning often occurs outside the realm of quantifiable data, in the spaces of embodied experience and intuitive understanding.

## Examples
* **Movement:** A dancer developing a unique style through improvisation and experimentation, rather than following a rigid curriculum.
* **Life:** A therapist gaining insight into a patient’s struggles through empathetic listening, rather than relying solely on diagnostic tests.
