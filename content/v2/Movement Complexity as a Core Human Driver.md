# Movement Complexity as a Core Human Driver

## Definition
Movement complexity refers to the principle that the human brain and nervous system evolved to process and coordinate increasingly intricate movement patterns, and that this complexity is fundamental to cognitive development, emotional well-being, and overall human flourishing.

## Explanation
Portale posits that the development of human intelligence is inextricably linked to our capacity for complex movement. He draws a parallel between mathematical learning, which progresses from simple addition to complex problem-solving, and the evolution of human movement.  Our ability to mimic animals, navigate diverse terrains, and engage in intricate activities like dance or martial arts demonstrates a unique level of movement complexity that distinguishes us. This isn't merely about physical prowess; it's about the neurological development that underlies it.  By challenging ourselves with increasingly complex movement patterns, we stimulate the brain, enhance cognitive function, and foster emotional resilience.  This aligns with embodied cognition theories, which emphasize the role of the body and movement in shaping thought and experience.

## Key Insight
The pursuit of movement complexity is not just about physical fitness; it's a pathway to cognitive and emotional growth, reflecting a core driver of human evolution.

## Examples
* **Movement:** Learning a new dance style, engaging in martial arts, or even rock climbing, all involve acquiring and integrating complex movement patterns.
* **Lack of Movement Complexity:** Repetitive, monotonous exercises that don't challenge the body's coordination and adaptability.

## Notes
The concept of movement complexity is easy to misunderstand as simply "doing harder exercises." It's about the *nature* of the movement, not necessarily its intensity.
