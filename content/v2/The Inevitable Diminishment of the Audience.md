# The Inevitable Diminishment of the Audience

## Definition
This concept describes the paradoxical relationship between depth of exploration and audience size, positing that as one delves deeper into a subject, the potential audience inevitably shrinks.

## Explanation
The speaker observes that their audience is becoming smaller as their practice becomes more specialized and unconventional. This isn’t a failure, but an inherent consequence of pursuing a deeper truth. The broader the appeal, the more compromises are necessary; the more radical the exploration, the more specialized the audience must become. This shrinking audience isn't a cause for regret, but a validation of the speaker's commitment to their vision. It represents a willingness to sacrifice popularity for authenticity, recognizing that true innovation often exists on the fringes. The speaker’s past pioneering work in areas like handstands and gymnastics demonstrates a willingness to push boundaries, but also acknowledges the inevitable narrowing of focus that comes with deeper exploration.

## Key Insight
Authentic exploration demands a willingness to sacrifice widespread appeal in pursuit of a deeper truth, inevitably leading to a smaller, more discerning audience.

## Examples
* **Textual:** The speaker's transition from teaching widely accessible movement skills to pursuing more esoteric practices.
* **Outside Movement:** A theoretical physicist whose work is understood only by a handful of specialists, compared to a popular science writer who simplifies complex concepts for a wider audience.
