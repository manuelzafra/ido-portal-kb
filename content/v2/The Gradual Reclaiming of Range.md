# The Gradual Reclaiming of Range

## Definition
Progress in restoring lost movement isn't about dramatic breakthroughs but rather a slow, iterative process of incremental gains, demanding constant self-assessment and adaptation.

## Explanation
The discussion regarding the squat challenge for individuals with pre-existing conditions (knee replacement, lower back issues) reveals a crucial principle: recovery and expansion of movement are rarely linear. Ido Portal emphasizes the need for individualized assessment and a cautious, gradual approach, starting with short durations (30 seconds) and modified positions (elevated squats). This highlights the body's sensitivity and the importance of paying close attention to feedback—inflammation and soreness signaling overexertion. This mirrors the broader concept of "minimum viable progress" in personal development, where small, consistent steps yield significant long-term results.

## Key Insight
Sustainable progress in movement, and in life, is built on the foundation of mindful, incremental adaptation.

## Examples
* **Movement:** Starting with 30 seconds of squats and gradually increasing the duration.
* **Outside Movement:** Learning a new language – consistent, short study sessions are more effective than infrequent, long cramming sessions.
