# The Paradox of the Shrinking Audience

## Definition
This concept highlights the counterintuitive relationship between depth of exploration and audience size: as one delves deeper into a subject, the audience inevitably diminishes, a necessary consequence of moving beyond the mainstream.

## Explanation
The speaker acknowledges the decreasing size of their audience as they pursue more unconventional approaches. This isn't viewed as a negative outcome, but as a natural consequence of prioritizing depth over popularity. The initial work of popularizing techniques like ring work and handstands served a purpose, but the speaker now seeks a different kind of development—both personal and for those willing to follow. This resonates with the broader phenomenon of niche expertise; the more specialized and challenging a field becomes, the fewer people will be equipped or motivated to engage with it. It’s a recognition that true innovation often exists on the fringes.

## Key Insight
The pursuit of profound understanding necessitates a willingness to embrace a smaller, more discerning audience.

## Examples
* **Scientific Breakthroughs:** Revolutionary scientific theories often initially face skepticism and limited acceptance before gaining wider recognition.
* **Avant-Garde Art:** Experimental art forms frequently attract a small but dedicated following, while remaining largely inaccessible to the general public.

## Notes
This isn't about intentionally alienating people, but about the unavoidable consequence of pursuing a path that deviates from the norm.
