# The Inevitable Shrinking Audience of Deep Inquiry

## Definition
As one delves deeper into complex or unconventional ideas, the audience capable of truly understanding and engaging with them inevitably diminishes. This isn't a failure, but a natural consequence of pursuing a path less traveled.

## Explanation
The speaker acknowledges the shrinking audience as a necessary byproduct of pursuing deeper inquiry. This isn't about popularity or marketability; it's about the inherent difficulty of grasping concepts that challenge fundamental assumptions. The initial phase of introducing new ideas often attracts a broader audience, but as the exploration intensifies, it requires a level of cognitive and emotional maturity that few possess. This shrinking audience isn't a loss, but a sign of fidelity to the inquiry itself – a willingness to prioritize depth over breadth. It’s a recognition that true understanding demands a commitment that few are willing to make.

## Key Insight
The pursuit of profound understanding inevitably leads to a smaller, more discerning audience.

## Examples
* **Textual Example:** "My audience is becoming smaller and smaller...the deeper and deeper you go, the smaller and smaller the audience, it has to happen."
* **Outside Movement:** The relatively small but devoted readership of complex philosophical works; the niche audience for experimental music.

## Notes
