# The Dissolution of Categorical Boundaries in Pursuit of the "Higher Thing"

## Definition
This concept describes a deliberate rejection of established categories and disciplines (like "movement culture" or "fitness") to pursue a deeper, undefined purpose – the "higher thing" – which transcends conventional understanding and language. It involves drawing from disparate fields (literature, movement, philosophy) and synthesizing them in unexpected ways.

## Explanation
The speaker actively resists being defined by existing frameworks, viewing them as potential constraints on exploration and development. This isn't about rebellion for its own sake, but a pragmatic necessity to access a more profound understanding and facilitate growth, both for the speaker and their students. The willingness to incorporate Borges and Dostoevsky into movement practice exemplifies this dissolution of boundaries; it's a recognition that practices and insights can be found in unexpected places. The inevitable shrinking audience is a consequence of this process – as one moves further from established norms, the pool of those willing to engage narrows. The "higher thing" isn’t a destination but a direction, a constant striving beyond the limits of current comprehension.

## Key Insight
True development requires a willingness to dismantle the categories that define us, even if it means sacrificing widespread appeal.

## Examples
* **Textual Example:** "But I am not bound by that. That does not interest me. I am free from all those definitions… And then I can do what I want."
* **Outside Movement:** A scientist drawing on art history to develop a new theory, or a musician incorporating elements of physics into their composition.
