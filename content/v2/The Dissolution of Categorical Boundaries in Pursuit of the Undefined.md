# The Dissolution of Categorical Boundaries in Pursuit of the Undefined

## Definition
This concept describes a deliberate rejection of pre-defined categories and disciplinary boundaries (like "movement culture" or "fitness practice") to pursue a deeper, undefined goal, prioritizing the needs of that pursuit above adherence to established norms. It's a process of actively dismantling limiting frameworks to access a more expansive understanding.

## Explanation
The speaker explicitly rejects being bound by conventional categories like "movement teacher" or "fitness practitioner," arguing that these constraints would prevent them from incorporating seemingly unrelated elements like Borges's writing. This isn’t about being contrarian for the sake of it; it’s a pragmatic necessity driven by a felt need – a desire to serve something beyond the scope of existing practices. The speaker acknowledges that this approach inevitably leads to a smaller audience, as deeper exploration necessitates a departure from mainstream appeal. This dissolution of boundaries isn't merely a stylistic choice; it's a fundamental epistemological shift, a way of *knowing* that prioritizes direct experience and need over adherence to established systems of knowledge. It suggests a belief that true growth, both personal and collective, requires a willingness to dismantle the structures that define it.

## Key Insight
True development necessitates a willingness to transcend pre-defined categories and embrace the discomfort of pursuing an undefined, potentially inaccessible, goal.

## Examples
* **Textual Example:** "But I am not bound by that. That does not interest me. I am free from all those definitions."
* **Outside Movement:** A scientist who disregards established theories to pursue a radical new hypothesis, even if it means facing ridicule and professional isolation.

## Notes
This concept is easily misinterpreted as mere eccentricity. The speaker’s rejection of categories isn’t arbitrary; it’s a tool for accessing something deeper, and the shrinking audience is a consequence of that pursuit.
