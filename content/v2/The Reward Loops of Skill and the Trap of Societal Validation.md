# The Reward Loops of Skill and the Trap of Societal Validation

## Definition
The pursuit of skill and mastery is often intertwined with external validation and reward loops, creating a system where individuals become instrumentalized by societal definitions of success and beauty, limiting their potential for genuine exploration and self-discovery.

## Explanation
The conversation explores how external validation, in the form of praise, recognition, or financial reward, can become a powerful force shaping our actions and limiting our potential. When we dedicate ourselves to developing a skill, the positive reinforcement we receive can create a feedback loop that encourages us to continue down a specific path, even if it's not aligned with our deepest desires. This can lead to a situation where we become "instrumentalized" – reduced to a tool that fulfills societal expectations rather than pursuing our own authentic growth. The lawyer example perfectly illustrates this: the pressure to be precise, logical, and aggressive can lead to a narrow definition of success, preventing exploration of alternative paths. Breaking free from these reward loops requires a conscious effort to question societal norms and prioritize intrinsic motivation over external validation.

## Key Insight
The pursuit of skill can be hijacked by external validation, leading to a narrow definition of success and limiting the potential for genuine self-discovery.

## Examples
* **Text Example:** The lawyer who becomes defined by their precision and aggression, limiting their potential for other forms of expression.
* **Outside Example:** A musician who prioritizes commercial success over artistic integrity, sacrificing their creative vision to appease record labels and audiences.
