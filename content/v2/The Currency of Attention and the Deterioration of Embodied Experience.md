# The Currency of Attention and the Deterioration of Embodied Experience

## Definition
This concept argues that a lack of focused attention and mindful presence in our physical activities and interactions with the world leads to a gradual deterioration of embodied experience, a loss of agency, and a diminished capacity for self-awareness and regulation.

## Explanation
The speakers connect the delegation of responsibility to external systems (like gyms or AI) with a broader societal trend of diminished attention and presence. Just as a neglected machine breaks down, the body and mind suffer when they are not actively engaged and attended to. This lack of attention leads to a loss of awareness of subtle bodily signals, a decline in motor control, and a susceptibility to chronic pain and dysfunction. The speaker emphasizes that attention is the "currency" that sustains our connection to our bodies and our ability to navigate the world effectively. The ability to be present and mindful, even in mundane activities, is crucial for maintaining a sense of agency and preventing the erosion of embodied experience.

## Key Insight
The consistent application of mindful attention is the essential nutrient for maintaining a vibrant and resilient embodied experience, preventing the insidious deterioration that arises from disengagement and delegation.

## Examples
* **Textual Example:** "If the body is not being attended to, it would break apart."
* **Outside Example:** Driving a car on autopilot, where one's attention is diverted, can lead to a loss of awareness of the surroundings and a diminished sense of control.
