# The Loss of Intuitive Movement: A Critique of Modern Parenting and Education

## Definition
This concept critiques the modern tendency to restrict and control children's movement, arguing that this inhibits their natural, intuitive movement patterns and ultimately leads to physical limitations and a disconnection from their bodies.

## Explanation
The speaker expresses frustration with parents and physiotherapists who discourage children from sitting in the "W position" on the floor, highlighting how this reflects a broader trend of overprotecting children and limiting their opportunities for natural movement exploration. This restriction, coupled with prolonged sitting in chairs, leads to a loss of mobility and flexibility, ultimately contributing to physical dysfunction later in life. The speaker contrasts this with the intuitive movement patterns of children and animals, who move freely and explore their bodies without constraint. This framework suggests that allowing children to move freely and experience discomfort is essential for developing a robust and adaptable physical foundation.

## Key Insight
Protecting children from discomfort and risk can inadvertently stifle their natural movement abilities and limit their potential for physical development.

## Examples
* **Movement:** A child being discouraged from sitting in a "W position" on the floor.
* **Outside Movement:** A parent constantly correcting a child's posture or gait.
