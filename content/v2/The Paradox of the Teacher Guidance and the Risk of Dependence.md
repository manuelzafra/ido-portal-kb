# The Paradox of the Teacher: Guidance and the Risk of Dependence

## Definition
The complex relationship between a student and a teacher, where guidance and support are essential for growth, but prolonged dependence can stifle autonomy and hinder true development.

## Explanation
The speaker acknowledges the vital role of teachers and mentors in guiding individuals towards deeper understanding and practice. However, he cautions against the potential for dependence, where the student becomes overly reliant on the teacher's direction and loses the ability to think and act independently. This creates a paradox: the very support that fosters initial growth can become a barrier to ultimate self-mastery. The speaker emphasizes the importance of eventually transcending the teacher's influence and developing one's own internal compass, a process that requires both humility and courage.  The analogy of the bicycle – initially needing guidance to assemble, but ultimately needing to be ridden independently – encapsulates this dynamic.

## Key Insight
True growth requires a delicate balance between guidance and autonomy, ultimately culminating in the ability to navigate one's own path.

## Examples
* **Textual Example:** "When you stay, you start to develop things. You start to cultivate things. When you don't, you have the illusion of developing, but you're shifting a lot of the things around."
* **Outside Example:** A child who constantly seeks their parent's approval, hindering their ability to make independent decisions and develop a sense of self.
