# The Developmental Arc: From Performance to Embodied Being

## Definition
This concept describes a potential trajectory of personal growth, moving from a phase of societal conformity and performance-driven goals to a stage of recognizing and integrating previously neglected aspects of the self, even if it means sacrificing a previously defined sense of success.

## Explanation
Portal describes a developmental arc where individuals initially navigate life by adhering to societal expectations and pursuing goals that define them within a specific framework. This phase often involves making compromises and suppressing certain aspects of their potential to achieve external validation and security. However, as individuals mature, they may experience a "call" to awaken these dormant potentialities, which requires a shift away from the performance-oriented mindset of the earlier phase. This transition can involve letting go of previously held identities and embracing a more expansive sense of self, even if it means becoming a "lesser version" of a narrowly defined success. This framework resonates with Jungian psychology's concept of individuation, where individuals integrate their shadow selves and move towards wholeness.

## Key Insight
True growth often requires a willingness to relinquish previously held identities and embrace a more expansive sense of self, even if it means sacrificing a conventional definition of success.

## Examples
* **Textual Example:** "We have to get humble. We have to Maybe breathe through the belly a little bit. We have to open ourselves. We have to become a beginner again at certain things."
* **Outside Movement:** A lawyer might leave a lucrative career to pursue a passion for art, or a scientist might abandon a promising research project to explore a more creative field.

## Notes
This concept highlights the importance of self-awareness and the courage to challenge societal expectations in order to pursue a more authentic and fulfilling life. It's a reminder that personal growth is not always linear and can involve periods of discomfort and uncertainty.
