# The Value of "Wordlessness" in Movement

## Definition
The practice of engaging in movement without verbal instruction or conscious thought, allowing for a more direct and intuitive connection to the body and its processes.

## Explanation
Ido Portal advocates for "non-verbal experiences" as a starting point for movement practice. This isn't about avoiding language entirely, but rather prioritizing direct sensory experience over intellectual analysis.  By minimizing verbal cues, individuals can bypass the filtering of the mind and access a more primal, embodied intelligence. This aligns with the idea that much of our knowledge and understanding is tacit – it exists beyond the capacity for explicit articulation.  The "clarification" that arises from this wordless practice isn't a logical understanding, but a felt sense of how the body moves and interacts with the world.

## Key Insight
Prioritizing non-verbal experience in movement practice unlocks a deeper, more intuitive understanding of the body and its capabilities.

## Examples
* **Text Example:** "One thing is what you call wordlessness. I have been recommending to people non-verbal experiences."
* **Outside Example:** A child learning to ride a bike. They don't need a lecture on physics; they learn through trial and error, guided by their senses and instincts.
