# The Teacher as Facilitator of Discovery

## Definition
A truly effective teacher doesn't simply impart knowledge; they create an environment and process that facilitates the student's own discovery and understanding, acting as a guide rather than a dispenser of information.

## Explanation
Portal’s view of teaching transcends the traditional model of instructor-led learning. He emphasizes the role of the teacher as a facilitator, someone who "unpacks the process" and helps students uncover their own potential. This approach aligns with constructivist learning theories, which prioritize active learning and the construction of knowledge through experience. The teacher's value lies not in what they *say*, but in how they *guide* the student's exploration. This requires a deep understanding of the student's individual needs and a willingness to relinquish control, allowing them to stumble, experiment, and ultimately, discover their own path. This concept has implications for parenting, mentorship, and leadership – all roles that involve guiding others toward growth.

## Key Insight
The greatest teachers don't fill vessels; they ignite fires.

## Examples
* **Movement:** A movement teacher who encourages experimentation and self-discovery, rather than rigidly prescribing techniques.
* **Life:** A mentor who provides guidance and support, but allows the mentee to make their own decisions and learn from their own mistakes.
