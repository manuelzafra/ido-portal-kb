# The Inevitable Shrinking Audience of Depth

## Definition
This concept describes the paradoxical relationship between the depth of one’s work and the size of the audience it attracts. As a practitioner delves deeper into complex and challenging material, the audience inevitably shrinks, as fewer individuals are willing or able to engage with it.

## Explanation
The speaker acknowledges a direct correlation between the depth of his practice and the diminishing size of his audience. This isn’t a lament, but an observation of a fundamental truth: profound work is inherently niche. The initial popularity gained through accessible practices (ring work, handstands) was a necessary stage, but it’s not sustainable for long-term growth.  The speaker's willingness to embrace this shrinking audience demonstrates a commitment to authenticity and a rejection of superficial appeal. It suggests a philosophical understanding that true value is not measured by popularity, but by the transformative impact on those who do engage. This mirrors the experience of any artist or thinker who pushes boundaries – the deeper they go, the more they isolate themselves from the mainstream.

## Key Insight
The pursuit of depth inevitably leads to a smaller, more discerning audience, and this is a necessary consequence of genuine innovation.

## Examples
* **Textual Example:** "My audience is becoming smaller and smaller… the deeper and deeper you go, the smaller and smaller the audience, it has to happen."
* **Outside Movement:** The relatively small but devoted readership of complex philosophical texts, or the niche audience for experimental music.
