# The "Garage" Mentality: Cultivating and Maintaining Systems

## Definition
This refers to the mindset of someone who not only understands how a system works but also possesses the ability to diagnose, repair, and improve it – a "garage" mechanic for movement or fighting.

## Explanation
The speaker uses the analogy of a garage mechanic to describe the role of traditional martial artists in relation to modern fighting. While fighters are focused on "driving" (performing), traditional martial artists are the ones who understand the underlying mechanics, capable of identifying and addressing weaknesses in the system. They possess a deep understanding of the fundamentals, allowing them to troubleshoot problems and optimize performance. This isn't about being a better fighter; it's about possessing a broader, more holistic understanding of movement and its application. It’s a recognition that true expertise lies not just in execution, but in the ability to diagnose and improve the system itself.

## Key Insight
True mastery involves not just performing a skill, but understanding and improving the underlying system that makes it possible.

## Examples
* **Text Example:** "The fighter needs to improve the carburetor in the car...The traditional martial artists...they fix cars, they develop pieces, shiny rods on the car, pimp my ride."
* **Outside Example:** A software engineer who not only writes code but also designs the architecture of the entire system.
