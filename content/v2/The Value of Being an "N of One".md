# The Value of Being an "N of One"

## Definition
This concept celebrates the importance of individual, idiosyncratic exploration and experimentation, rejecting standardized approaches in favor of a personalized, iterative process of discovery.

## Explanation
The speaker’s description of being an “N of one” – a scientific term referring to a sample size of one – highlights the value of unique, personalized approaches to practice and learning. It’s a rejection of the tendency to follow established methods blindly, and an endorsement of evolving and creating new practices based on individual experience. This aligns with a broader emphasis on embodied cognition and the understanding that knowledge is not passively received but actively constructed through interaction with the world. The praise for the speaker’s willingness to “embrace existing practices, evolve them, create new practices” underscores the importance of individual agency and the potential for innovation that arises from challenging conventional wisdom. It’s a call to embrace one’s own unique journey, even if it deviates from the norm.

## Key Insight
True progress and understanding emerge not from conformity to established methods, but from the courage to explore and innovate as an individual.

## Examples
* **Textual Example:** "And you truly are. I don't think there's anyone that has been as willing to embrace existing practices, evolve them, create new practices, and to share so broadly..."
* **Outside Movement:** A chef who experiments with unconventional ingredients and techniques to create a unique culinary experience. A musician who blends different genres to develop a distinctive sound.

## Notes
This concept is not about advocating for reckless experimentation. It’s about approaching practice with a spirit of inquiry and a willingness to adapt and innovate based on individual experience and feedback.
