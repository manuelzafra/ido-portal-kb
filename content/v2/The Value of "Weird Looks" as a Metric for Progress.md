# The Value of "Weird Looks" as a Metric for Progress

## Definition
This unconventional metric suggests that discomfort and social deviation—indicated by receiving "weird looks"—serve as a signal that one is venturing beyond established norms and approaching a deeper, more authentic understanding.

## Explanation
The speaker proposes that the social discomfort arising from unconventional practices isn't a sign of failure, but rather a validation of progress. It implies that true innovation and growth often require challenging established conventions and venturing into uncharted territory. This resonates with the idea that groundbreaking discoveries are often met with skepticism and resistance. The "weird looks" represent the friction that arises when one's actions deviate from the accepted norm, indicating a departure from the superficial and a movement towards a more profound truth. It's a playful yet insightful way to encourage pushing boundaries.

## Key Insight
Social discomfort can be a valuable compass, guiding one towards a more authentic and transformative experience.

## Examples
* **Text Example:** "If you don't get the weird looks, you're not moving in the right direction."
* **Outside Example:** A scientist proposing a radical new theory might face ridicule and skepticism, but this resistance can be a sign that they are challenging the status quo and pushing the boundaries of knowledge.
