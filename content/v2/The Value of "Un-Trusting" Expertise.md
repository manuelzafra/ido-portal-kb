# The Value of "Un-Trusting" Expertise

## Definition
A pedagogical approach that encourages questioning and challenging established authority and expertise, even when it appears to be well-intentioned or beneficial. This fosters independent thinking and a deeper understanding through direct observation and experimentation.

## Explanation
Mark’s interaction with the man walking on rails, initially accepting the local method before questioning and then being proven wrong, illustrates the importance of “un-trusting” expertise. It's not about rejecting all authority, but about maintaining a critical and inquisitive mindset. The willingness to challenge assumptions, even when they are deeply ingrained in a culture, is essential for genuine learning and adaptation. His subsequent correction of the man's gait demonstrates a commitment to observation and experimentation over blind acceptance. This principle extends beyond movement; it’s a broader call for intellectual humility and a rejection of dogma. The fact that he corrected someone in front of elders and shamans highlights the courage required to challenge established norms.

## Key Insight
True learning requires a willingness to question and challenge even seemingly authoritative knowledge, fostering independent thinking and deeper understanding.

## Examples
* **Walking on Rails:** Mark initially accepting the local method of walking on rails before questioning and being proven wrong.
* **Challenging Medical Advice:** Mark’s decision to disregard his sister’s orthopedic surgeon’s advice regarding his broken finger.
* **Questioning Movement Experts:** Mark’s skepticism towards Instagram influencers who present themselves as movement experts.

## Notes
This concept is often misinterpreted as advocating for disrespect towards experts. It’s about fostering a culture of inquiry and encouraging individuals to think for themselves, even when it means challenging authority.
