# The Safe Method is a Useless Method

## Definition
This concept argues that prioritizing safety above all else can actually hinder progress and limit movement potential.

## Explanation
While injury prevention is important, an excessive focus on avoiding risk can stifle exploration, experimentation, and the development of resilience.

## Key Insight
True movement mastery requires embracing a degree of risk and discomfort, allowing the body to adapt and strengthen through controlled challenges.

## Examples
*   Louis Simmons’s observation that “the safest exercise probably aren’t great for strength.”

## Notes
This concept challenges the conventional wisdom of prioritizing safety above all else. It’s a reminder that growth often requires stepping outside of one’s comfort zone.
