# 1. Movement Culture: The Foundation of Practice

## Definition
Movement culture refers to the social context and interactions surrounding movement practices, arguing that movement cannot exist without this supportive community and shared understanding.

## Explanation
Ido Portal posits that movement isn't just about individual physical feats; it’s fundamentally shaped by the people involved and the way they interact. This “culture” provides the motivation, feedback, and shared understanding necessary for sustained practice and growth. It’s about the group dynamic, not just the individual movement.

## Key Insight
Movement is inherently social; its success depends on the collective experience and shared understanding within a movement culture.

## Examples
The example of Conor McGregor’s journey highlights how a supportive movement culture can foster extraordinary growth and mentorship.

* Opposes: [[Isolated Practice]]

## Notes
This concept emphasizes the importance of community and shared experience in driving movement practice, moving beyond a purely technical or individualistic approach.
