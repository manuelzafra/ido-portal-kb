# The Role of the Eyes

## Definition
The eyes play a fundamental role in movement practice, serving as a gateway to deeper awareness and influencing the body’s internal organization.

## Explanation
This concept highlights the importance of paying attention to the way we use our eyes during movement, recognizing that they can significantly impact our experience and performance.

## Key Insight
The eyes are a primary entry point for accessing deeper levels of awareness and influencing the body’s internal organization.

## Examples
Ido’s observation of how tilting the head can immediately affect the organization of the feet, and his emphasis on exploring different visual perspectives.
