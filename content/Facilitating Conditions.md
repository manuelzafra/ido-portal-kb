# <Facilitating Conditions>

## Definition
Creating an environment or set of circumstances that naturally encourages a desired behavior or outcome, often through subtle cues and allowing for spontaneous action rather than imposing rigid structures.

## Explanation
This involves removing obstacles, creating opportunities, and subtly guiding behavior without direct instruction or control. It’s about designing the context for success, trusting the individual’s agency, and letting the process unfold organically.

## Key Insight
“How do I get this to work versus how do I make it work?” – Ido Portal

## Examples
*   The open door in the story – creating a space for spontaneous movement.
*   The Shilajit water – a system that requires ongoing attention and care, facilitating a deeper understanding of responsibility.
