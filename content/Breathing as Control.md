# Breathing as Control

## Definition
Utilizing conscious breathing patterns to influence nervous system state, promoting relaxation, focus, and control during movement.

## Explanation
This concept highlights the connection between breath and movement. By consciously controlling the breath – specifically, favoring nasal breathing – individuals can influence their nervous system state, reducing stress and promoting a more focused and controlled movement response. It’s about using the breath as a tool to manage the nervous system's reactivity.

## Key Insight
Breath is a fundamental regulator of the nervous system, profoundly impacting movement quality and control.

## Examples
*   [SPEAKER_03]’s emphasis on breathing through the nose to minimize sound and create a more controlled state.
*   The instruction to “keep breathing” throughout the stick-catching exercise, reinforcing the importance of breath control.

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Limbic Friction]], [[Parasympathetic Nervous System]]
*   Opposes: [[Hyperventilation]] (Rapid, shallow breathing that can exacerbate anxiety and impair movement)

## Notes
*   This concept aligns with broader principles of movement physiology, recognizing the significant role of the autonomic nervous system in movement control.
