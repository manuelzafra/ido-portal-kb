# The “Rebel” Response to Restriction

## Definition
The instinctive, often resistant, reaction to imposed limitations or restrictions on movement, driven by a fundamental desire for freedom and exploration.

## Explanation
This concept acknowledges that restrictions – whether physical, social, or psychological – can trigger a powerful urge to push boundaries, challenge authority, and seek out alternative ways of moving. It’s a recognition of the human need for autonomy and the inherent drive to explore the limits of one’s capabilities.

## Key Insight
Restrictions often elicit a “rebel” response, fueling creativity, innovation, and a desire for freedom.
