# The Nervous System as the Final Common Path

## Definition
This concept, popularized by Nobel laureate Sherrington, posits that a vast majority of the nervous system’s functions are dedicated to movement, serving as the primary pathway for sensory input and motor output.

## Explanation
Sherrington’s observation that the nervous system is overwhelmingly focused on movement suggests that movement isn’t a separate function but the foundational process upon which all other nervous system activities are built. It’s the central nervous system’s primary job.

## Key Insight
Movement is not a peripheral activity but the fundamental operating system of the nervous system, driving a significant portion of its complexity and capabilities.

## Examples
*   Sherrington’s original observation regarding the extensive neural pathways dedicated to movement.
