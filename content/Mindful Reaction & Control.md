# Mindful Reaction & Control

## Definition
The ability to consciously modulate movement responses, resisting automatic reactions and instead choosing a deliberate action based on perceived sensory input.

## Explanation
This concept highlights the distinction between instinctive reactions and controlled, intentional movements. It’s about recognizing the urge to react – whether it’s accelerating, overcorrecting, or simply falling into a habitual pattern – and actively choosing a different response. It’s about gaining control over the nervous system’s automatic responses.

## Key Insight
Movement is fundamentally a process of controlled reaction, where awareness and intention guide action.
