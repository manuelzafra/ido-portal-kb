# 3. The Mirror & Distortion

## Definition
Our perception of ourselves is heavily influenced by the people around us, and if those influences are flawed (jealous, shameful, distorted), our self-image will be similarly compromised.

## Explanation
We need “mirrors” – individuals who can reflect us accurately – to develop a healthy sense of self. However, if these mirrors are tainted, they create a distorted view, leading to self-doubt and insecurity.

## Key Insight
Authentic self-discovery requires actively seeking out and engaging with honest, unjudgmental perspectives.

## Examples
(From transcript) “If you don't see who we are. So a teacher, we talked about this with Tim, parents, teachers, there’s people around us that we need them to mirror us accurately.” – This illustrates the core principle.
