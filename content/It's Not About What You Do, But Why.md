# It's Not About What You Do, But Why

## Definition
This concept emphasizes the importance of understanding the underlying motivations and intentions behind movement practices, rather than simply focusing on the technical execution.

## Explanation
The “why” – the deeper purpose and meaning behind the movement – can be just as, if not more, important than the “how” – the specific techniques and strategies employed.

## Key Insight
Genuine movement progress is driven by a commitment to self-discovery, personal growth, and a deeper connection to the body.

## Examples
*   Ido Portal’s questioning of the motivations behind movement practices, seeking to understand the underlying needs and desires.

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Movement as Investigation]]
*   Opposes: [[Technique-Driven Training]]

## Notes
This concept highlights the importance of intrinsic motivation in movement. It’s a reminder that movement should be driven by purpose, not by external pressures or expectations.
