# The Attempt to Solve It is Enough

## Definition
This concept suggests that the act of engaging with a complex movement challenge, even without achieving a definitive solution, holds intrinsic value and contributes to personal growth.

## Explanation
The pursuit of a “perfect” answer can be paralyzing. The willingness to grapple with the difficulty, to experiment, and to learn from mistakes is itself a valuable process.

## Key Insight
The journey of exploration and adaptation is more important than arriving at a fixed, predetermined outcome.

## Examples
*   Ido Portal’s focus on the “attempt to solve it” as a worthwhile goal, even if a perfect solution remains elusive.

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Movement as Investigation]]
*   Opposes: [[Goal-Oriented Performance]]

## Notes
This concept encourages a shift in mindset from outcome-focused performance to process-oriented learning. It’s about embracing the inherent uncertainty of movement.
