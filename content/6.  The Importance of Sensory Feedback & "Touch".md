# 6.  The Importance of Sensory Feedback & "Touch"

## Definition
The critical role of tactile feedback – the sense of touch – in learning and refining movement patterns.

## Explanation
This concept emphasizes the value of actively engaging with the body’s sensations during movement, rather than relying solely on visual cues or abstract concepts.

## Key Insight
“You wouldn't want to spend a week with me with my personal practice. It would be painful, boring, difficult in ways that you wouldn't want.” – A recognition of the intensity and focus required for effective sensory exploration.

## Examples
The use of a tennis ball to stimulate tactile feedback.

## Relationships
* Related to: [[Proprioception]], [[Kinesthetic Learning]]
* Opposes: [[Visual-Only Learning]]

## Notes
This highlights the importance of embodied learning and the role of the body in shaping our understanding of movement.
