# 4. Practice as Embodiment – The Bridge to Living

## Definition
This concept emphasizes that “practice” isn’t a separate activity, but rather the *process* of integrating ideas and principles into daily life through physical and mental engagement.

## Explanation
Ido Portal rejects the notion of “practice” as a purely technical exercise. It’s about actively applying the insights gained through movement, meditation, or other practices to every aspect of one’s experience – constantly testing and refining one’s understanding through action.

## Key Insight
True transformation happens when practice is not just a set of techniques, but a way of *living*.

## Examples
*   Modulating emotions through loving-kindness meditation while experiencing a challenging situation.
*   Regularly engaging in physical movement to test and refine one’s focus and concentration.

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Action-Oriented Learning]], [[Integration]]
*   Opposes: [[Passive Learning]]

## Notes
