# 5. Traditional Martial Arts as a Source of Movement Wisdom

## Definition
Traditional martial arts, particularly internal styles like Tai Chi and Gong Fu, offer valuable insights into movement principles, body mechanics, and the relationship between movement and breath.

## Explanation
This concept argues that traditional martial arts, often dismissed as purely combative, contain a wealth of knowledge about human movement that can be applied to a wide range of activities, including sports and movement training.

## Key Insight
“Most traditional martial artists are actually movement people that they would love to actually do what I do.” –  Recognizing the inherent movement principles within traditional martial arts.

## Examples
*   The focus on breathing with movement in Tai Chi and Gong Fu.
*   The emphasis on shaping the body during movement, rather than simply generating force.
