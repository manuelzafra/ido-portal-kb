# The Capacity to Know What’s Important

## Definition
The ability to discern valuable pursuits and activities from superficial or distracting ones, developed through sustained engagement and reflection within a core practice.

## Explanation
This concept describes the gradual development of discernment – the ability to identify what truly matters and to prioritize those things accordingly. It’s not a sudden revelation, but a slow, deliberate process of learning through experience and reflection.

## Key Insight
Discernment emerges through sustained engagement and reflection within a meaningful practice.

## Examples
*   A practitioner gradually recognizing which aspects of their practice are most beneficial and which are simply time-wasting.
*   A student developing a deeper understanding of the core principles of a discipline through consistent study and application.

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Intentionality]], [[Self-Awareness]], [[The Process of Learning]]
*   Opposes: [[Impulsivity]], [[Distraction]]

## Notes
This highlights the importance of patience and persistence in developing discernment. It’s a reminder that true understanding doesn’t come overnight.
