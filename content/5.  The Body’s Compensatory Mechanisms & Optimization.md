# 5.  The Body’s Compensatory Mechanisms & Optimization

## Definition
The distinction between compensatory systems (addressing leaks and maintaining stability) and optimization systems (achieving maximal fluidity and efficiency).

## Explanation
This concept highlights the difference between simply “patching up” a weakness and actually improving the underlying movement pattern. Compensatory systems are reactive, while optimization systems are proactive.
