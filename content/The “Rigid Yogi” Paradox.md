# The “Rigid Yogi” Paradox

## Definition
Despite the perceived mobility and grace often associated with yoga practitioners, a closer examination reveals that many yogis exhibit significant rigidity and limited micro-movement within their joints, particularly in the spine and rib cage.

## Explanation
This concept challenges the common perception of yogis as inherently “mobile.” It highlights that many yogis, due to specific postural training, develop a degree of stiffness and restriction in their movement patterns, particularly in the smaller, more subtle movements that are crucial for optimal joint health and function.

## Key Insight
Mobility is not solely defined by large, impressive movements; it’s also characterized by the ability to generate and control small, precise movements within the joints.

## Examples
*   A yogi performing a challenging asana with seemingly effortless grace while exhibiting significant restriction in the micro-movements between the vertebrae.
*   A therapist observing a yogi’s movement and identifying a lack of subtle rib cage rotation and movement.
