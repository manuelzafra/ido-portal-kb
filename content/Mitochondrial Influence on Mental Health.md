# Mitochondrial Influence on Mental Health

## Definition
Mental health is directly influenced by the accumulation of “friction” within the body’s mitochondrial systems, stemming from interactions with the environment.

## Explanation
This concept, popularized by Dr. Christopher Palmer, posits that our thoughts and emotions aren’t simply abstract mental phenomena, but are generated within the body’s energy-producing organelles (mitochondria).  The constant processing of experiences creates “friction” within these systems, impacting mental states.

## Key Insight
Mental and emotional well-being are fundamentally linked to the physical health and efficiency of our mitochondrial function.

## Examples
The rhythmic movements of dance, sex, or rowing – activities that naturally induce a state of “feeling good” – are beneficial not just for physical sensation, but for reducing this “friction” within the body’s energy systems.
