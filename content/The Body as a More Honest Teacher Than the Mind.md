# The Body as a More Honest Teacher Than the Mind

## Definition
This concept suggests that the body, through its direct experience of movement and sensation, offers a more reliable and truthful source of knowledge than the analytical, conceptual mind.

## Explanation
Our minds are prone to biases, assumptions, and self-deception. The body, on the other hand, is unfiltered and immediate. When we pay attention to the sensations arising within our bodies during movement, we bypass the mental chatter and access a deeper, more intuitive understanding of what’s actually happening.

## Key Insight
Trusting your body’s feedback is more valuable than relying on intellectual analysis or external instruction.

## Examples
*   A runner ignoring pain signals and pushing through an injury, ultimately leading to further damage.
*   A dancer intuitively adjusting their movement based on the feeling of balance and flow, rather than consciously calculating their angles and positions.
