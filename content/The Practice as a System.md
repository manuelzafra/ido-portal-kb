# The Practice as a System

## Definition
A structured approach to continuous learning and development, encompassing not just specific movements or skills, but also the underlying principles of awareness, responsiveness, and self-discovery.

## Explanation
This concept emphasizes that “practice” isn’t simply about repetition, but about cultivating a system of continuous learning and growth. It involves intentionally designing practices that challenge assumptions, promote embodied awareness, and foster a deeper understanding of oneself and the world.

## Key Insight
A truly effective practice is a dynamic and evolving system, constantly adapting to new experiences and insights.

## Examples
The speaker outlines "official" practices (structured routines) and “unofficial” practices (allowing for spontaneous exploration and experimentation) – recognizing that both are essential components of a robust system.

## Relationships
* Related to: [[Deliberate Practice]], [[Growth Mindset]]
* Opposes: [[Passive Habit Formation]] (where behaviors are simply ingrained without conscious intention)

## Notes
This concept highlights the importance of creating sustainable habits and routines that support long-term growth and transformation.
