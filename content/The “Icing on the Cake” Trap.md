# 2. The “Icing on the Cake” Trap

## Definition
This describes the tendency to prioritize superficial achievements (like social media validation) over deeper, more fundamental mastery and understanding.

## Explanation
It’s a critique of focusing on external rewards or appearances (the “icing”) without addressing the underlying skill or knowledge (the core). It’s about recognizing that chasing external validation can distract from genuine progress.

## Key Insight
“That’s what easily presentable on social media.” – External validation often obscures true mastery.

## Examples
Speaker [00]’s initial observation about boxing wins achieved without a punch thrown exemplifies this trap.
