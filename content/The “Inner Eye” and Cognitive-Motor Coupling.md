# 3. The “Inner Eye” and Cognitive-Motor Coupling

## Definition
The interconnectedness between visual perception and internal movement processes, where visual input directly influences and shapes motor execution.

## Explanation
This concept proposes that our visual experience isn’t just a passive recording of the external world; it actively participates in shaping our movement. The “inner eye” represents this internal representation of movement, influenced by visual input.

## Key Insight
Visual perception and motor execution are deeply intertwined, with visual input directly shaping internal movement representations.

## Examples
*   The metaphorical description of the eyes as a “representation of the way that we use various cognitive and mind processes.”

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Cognitive Control]], [[Feedback Loops]]
*   Opposes: [[Disconnection of Senses]]

## Notes
This concept underscores the dynamic relationship between perception and action, suggesting a continuous feedback loop.
