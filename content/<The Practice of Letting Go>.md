# <The Practice of Letting Go>

## Definition
A deliberate act of relinquishing control, expectation, or the need for a predetermined outcome, allowing for the natural flow of movement, learning, and experience.

## Explanation
This involves accepting uncertainty, trusting the process, and letting go of the urge to force a specific result. It’s about embracing the inherent messiness of life and allowing for unexpected discoveries.

## Key Insight
“I want to move here. I just let go of something and my body moves here.” – Ido Portal

## Examples
*   The movement with the body – letting go of the intention to perform a specific movement and allowing the body to respond organically.
*   The decision about having children – accepting the unknown and trusting the unfolding of life.

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Non-Attachment]], [[Flow State]]
*   Opposes: [[Control]], [[Resistance]]

## Notes
This is a core principle of Ido’s movement practice, emphasizing the importance of surrender and trust in the process.
