# 10.  The “Metabolic Stomach” – Constant Harvesting of Information

## Definition
This concept describes a continuous process of absorbing and integrating new information through experience and interaction.

## Explanation
It’s about recognizing that knowledge isn’t passively received but actively created through engagement with the world. The “metabolic stomach” represents the body’s ability to process and transform information into wisdom and understanding.

## Key Insight
“That is the point. And I never did this work. Because I didn't need to. Because there was nothing special about it. why this work feels special is because of deficiencies in your state and approach and that is why it was felt powerful and that’s why I brought it to you guys.”

## Examples
The speaker’s own practice of constantly experimenting and adapting his approach.
