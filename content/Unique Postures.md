# Unique Postures

## Definition
The core of movement practice lies in recognizing that fundamental postures – whether physical, mental, or emotional – are shared across individuals and persist throughout life.

## Explanation
This concept suggests that despite variations in movement styles and techniques, certain underlying postural patterns are common to all human movement. Recognizing these patterns allows for a deeper understanding of movement and a more efficient approach to practice.

## Key Insight
Movement patterns are rooted in fundamental, enduring postural configurations that remain consistent throughout a person's life.

## Examples
Ido’s observation that people who learn new sports or techniques often unconsciously adopt the same postural patterns they’ve previously developed.
