# The Examination as a Foundational Practice

## Definition
Movement practice should initially prioritize open-ended exploration and investigation over the pursuit of specific outcomes or techniques.

## Explanation
The initial phase of movement practice should be dedicated to a process of careful observation and experimentation, focusing on generating new sensory data and challenging pre-conceived notions rather than attempting to achieve a predetermined goal.

## Key Insight
The primary purpose of movement practice is to cultivate a capacity for open-minded exploration and self-discovery.

## Examples
Trying a movement without a specific intention, focusing solely on the sensations and movement qualities that arise.

## Relationships
* Related to: [[Curiosity-Driven Learning]], [[Process-Oriented Practice]]
* Opposes: [[Goal-Oriented Practice]]

## Notes
This emphasizes the importance of letting the movement itself guide the practice, rather than imposing external goals or expectations.
