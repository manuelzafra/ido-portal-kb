# 6. The Body as a Primary Guide

## Definition
This concept suggests that the body's internal signals and sensations are a more reliable source of information than the intellect or conscious thought, particularly when pursuing complex goals.

## Explanation
It’s about prioritizing bodily awareness and intuitive responses over rigid plans or external pressures. The body knows what it needs, even if the mind doesn't.

## Key Insight
“The body doesn't lie as much as the head.” –  Trusting the body’s wisdom can lead to more effective and sustainable action.

## Examples
*   An athlete modifying their training based on how their body feels.
*   A person adjusting their diet based on how their body responds to different foods.

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Somatic Awareness]], [[Intuition]]
*   Opposes: [[Cognitive Bias]]

## Notes
This concept draws on insights from practices like yoga and somatic experiencing.
