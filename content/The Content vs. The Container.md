# 6. The Content vs. The Container

## Definition
Distinguishing between the specific skills or techniques learned (the “content”) and the broader context, mindset, and approach to learning (the “container”).

## Explanation
Ido uses this metaphor to explain that movement isn’t just about mastering a particular skill like a handstand; it’s about the entire framework of how you approach learning, adapting, and embracing the unknown.  It's about the way you *do* the movement, not just the movement itself.

## Key Insight
A successful movement practice requires a flexible and adaptable “container” that can accommodate both skill acquisition and a willingness to embrace uncertainty.

## Examples
The discussion about adapting to unexpected challenges and maintaining a growth mindset illustrates this concept.
