# 2. Utilizing Opposites: The River & The Current

## Definition
Movement should not be solely about resisting external forces (like gravity) but about intelligently utilizing them – harnessing the “current” like a river.

## Explanation
This moves beyond a simple “push-and-pull” dynamic. It’s about recognizing that forces aren’t inherently antagonistic; they can be leveraged for movement and structure.

## Key Insight
Movement is a process of harmonizing with opposing forces, not fighting them.

## Examples
The river analogy – swimming *with* the current, not against it.

* Opposes: [[Rigid Resistance]]
