# 2. The Importance of Embodied Awareness

## Definition
The ability to deeply sense and understand one’s body’s movements, sensations, and limitations in real-time is crucial for preventing injury and optimizing performance.

## Explanation
This concept emphasizes the need for active, present engagement with the body, moving beyond passive adherence to external programs or trends. It’s about developing a “felt sense” of movement and its impact.

## Key Insight
Movement is not just a mechanical action, but a conscious, embodied experience.

## Examples
*   Paying attention to the subtle shifts in posture and alignment during a squat, rather than simply focusing on the weight lifted.

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Sensorimotor Integration]], [[Presence]]
*   Opposes: [[Passive Training]]

## Notes
This concept is central to Portal’s approach to movement and learning – a return to a more intuitive and embodied way of training.
