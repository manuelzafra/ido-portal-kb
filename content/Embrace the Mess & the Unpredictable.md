# Embrace the Mess & the Unpredictable

## Definition
True learning and adaptation arise from embracing uncertainty, discomfort, and the inevitable “mess” that occurs when pushing beyond established boundaries.

## Explanation
This advocates for resisting the urge to control every aspect of a movement experience and instead, allowing for spontaneous responses and deviations from a pre-determined plan.  It’s about accepting failure as a necessary part of the learning process and using it as a catalyst for growth.

## Key Insight
The most valuable learning opportunities often emerge from navigating unpredictable and challenging situations.

## Examples
*   The expedition into the Indonesian jungle – where the group’s initial plans were repeatedly disrupted by the terrain and the tribe’s unconventional methods.
*   The boxer Vasily Machenko – whose movement is unconstrained by ego or preconceived notions.
