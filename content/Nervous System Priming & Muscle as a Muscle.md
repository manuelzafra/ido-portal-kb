# Nervous System Priming & Muscle as a Muscle

## Definition
The nervous system’s response to movement is shaped by daily habits; actively seeking novel and stimulating experiences can “prime” the nervous system for greater adaptability and resilience.

## Explanation
This suggests that our nervous system becomes accustomed to specific patterns of movement and sensory input. By intentionally introducing new and varied experiences, we can disrupt these established patterns and unlock greater potential for movement capacity.  Treating the body like a muscle – requiring consistent, varied stimulation – is key.

## Key Insight
Regularly challenging the nervous system through diverse movement experiences can dramatically increase adaptability and resilience.

## Examples
*   The expedition into the Indonesian jungle – exposing the body to radically different terrain and movement demands.
*   The boxer Vasily Machenko – whose movement approach is unconstrained by ego or preconceived notions.
