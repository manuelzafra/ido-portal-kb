# 4. Maintenance as a Conscious Choice, Not Neglect

## Definition
Skill maintenance isn't a passive process of simply repeating a movement; it’s a deliberate and conscious choice to continue developing and refining a skill, driven by a clear understanding of its value and purpose.

## Explanation
This concept emphasizes that maintenance is not simply a routine or habit. It requires a conscious decision to continue practicing and developing a skill, based on a clear understanding of its value and purpose.

## Key Insight
Maintenance is a proactive choice, not a passive habit, and should be driven by a clear understanding of its value.

## Examples
*   “Let it go with a choice. Like I said, if a muscle was lost, I tried it to be a choice.” – This highlights the importance of making a conscious decision to maintain a skill.
*   The deliberate effort to continue practicing a skill, even when it’s not immediately useful, demonstrating a commitment to long-term development.

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Intentional Practice]], [[Growth Mindset]]
*   Opposes: [[Passive Habit]]

## Notes
This shifts the focus from simply "doing" to actively *choosing* to maintain a skill, emphasizing the importance of intention and purpose.
