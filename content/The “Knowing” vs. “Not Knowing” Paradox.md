# 5.  The “Knowing” vs. “Not Knowing” Paradox

## Definition
This concept describes the shift from a fixed, pre-determined understanding of a movement to an embrace of the unknown and the potential for discovery through playful investigation.

## Explanation
Instead of adhering to a specific sequence or technique, the practice involves intentionally stepping outside of established knowledge and exploring the possibilities within the unknown. It’s about acknowledging the limits of one’s understanding and allowing for spontaneous adaptation.

## Key Insight
True mastery comes not from knowing *how* to do something, but from the willingness to *explore* what’s possible.

## Examples
*   “Like in a yoga practice it's often there, like I know what I need to do.” – Speaker 01
*   The contrast between a rigid protocol and a playful investigation.
