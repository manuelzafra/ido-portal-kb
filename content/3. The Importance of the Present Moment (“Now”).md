# 3. The Importance of the Present Moment (“Now”)

## Definition
The present moment – often referred to as “now” – is the only reality we can truly access. It’s a dynamic, unfolding process, distinct from fixed memories of the past and projections of the future.

## Explanation
This emphasizes the significance of fully inhabiting the present. It’s not simply about mindfulness, but about actively recognizing and engaging with the constant flux of experience, resisting the urge to dwell on the past or anticipate the future.

## Key Insight
“Being now, living now, not just existing and having that as a primary interface to who I am.” – A call to actively engage with the present as the locus of experience and self-definition.

## Examples
*   The focus on the physical sensations of the horse stance – a grounding in the immediate experience of the body.
*   The concept of “untouchable” present – a moment that cannot be grasped or controlled.

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Mindfulness]], [[Present Moment Awareness]]
*   Opposes: [[Rumination]], [[Worry]]

## Notes
*   This concept is closely tied to the idea of “flow state,” where attention is fully absorbed in the present activity.
