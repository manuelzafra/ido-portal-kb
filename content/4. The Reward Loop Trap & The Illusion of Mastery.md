# 4. The Reward Loop Trap & The Illusion of Mastery

## Definition
This describes a common psychological phenomenon where the pursuit of rewards (e.g., recognition, achievement) can actually hinder genuine progress and create a rigid, limiting mindset.

## Explanation
When we become overly focused on external rewards, we tend to optimize our behavior for those rewards, often at the expense of deeper learning and exploration. This can lead to a narrow, specialized approach where we become “stuck” in a particular role or skill set, unable to adapt or grow.

## Key Insight
Genuine mastery comes from intrinsic motivation and a willingness to embrace discomfort and uncertainty, not from chasing external validation.

## Examples
*   “You're again limiting yourself, you're again setting yourself into traps. For me, it was being a lawyer, always being very precise and very logical and aggressive and being connected to these different outcomes. And the world, like the market, loved a certain version of me that was very good at achieving certain outcomes. And then I got caught up in those reward loops. And then it was like, okay, more, more, more, more, more.”
