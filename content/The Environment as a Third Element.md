# The Environment as a Third Element

## Definition
The environment – encompassing external stimuli and conditions – represents a crucial third element in the framework of movement, influencing and being influenced by the nervous system and the body.

## Explanation
This perspective expands beyond the traditional focus on the body’s internal mechanics (core and skeletal system) and the nervous system, recognizing the significant role of the external environment in shaping movement patterns and experiences.

## Key Insight
The environment is a vital third element in understanding movement, influencing and being influenced by the nervous system and the body.

## Examples
Ido’s description of Moshe Feldenkrais’s concept of the environment as a unique and dynamic element.

* Opposes: [[Isolated Body Focus]]

## Notes
This concept encourages a more holistic approach to movement, recognizing the interconnectedness between the body, the mind, and the environment.
