# 2. Choosing Love – Beyond Possession

## Definition
This concept proposes that “love” as traditionally understood (often romanticized) is frequently rooted in possessive desires and self-serving gratification. True love, according to Portal, requires a conscious choice to engage with another being, regardless of the potential for discomfort or disruption.

## Explanation
The transcript argues that romantic love, in its conventional form, is often a distorted expression of desire, driven by a need for control and validation.  Portal advocates for a more fundamental “choosing” of love – a deliberate act of connection that transcends the immediate emotional gratification. This choice is not dependent on the other person’s “worthiness” or the fulfillment of pre-conceived notions.

## Key Insight
“Love is nothing without choice” – the act of consciously choosing to love, even in the face of difficulty, is the essential element.

## Examples
The fisherman’s love for his fish, driven by pleasure, is contrasted with the mother’s love, which is rooted in the desire for her child’s growth and development.
