# The Importance of Examining the Body’s “Engine”

## Definition
This concept refers to the exploration of the body’s internal movement patterns, particularly those related to the spine and its ability to generate movement through undulating and rotational motions.

## Explanation
It suggests that focusing solely on external movements can be limiting and that understanding the underlying mechanics of the body’s movement engine can unlock greater potential and adaptability.

## Key Insight
The body’s internal movement patterns are just as important as external movements.

## Examples
*   Exploring spinal waves to improve movement fluidity and coordination.

## Relationships

## Notes
This concept highlights the importance of internal awareness and the potential for restoring natural movement patterns.
