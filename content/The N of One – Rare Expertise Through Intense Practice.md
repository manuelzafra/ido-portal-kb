# The N of One – Rare Expertise Through Intense Practice

## Definition
The “N of one” concept refers to an individual who has achieved a level of expertise through exceptionally focused and intense practice, representing a unique and rare case study.

## Explanation
It’s a statistical term used to describe someone who has accumulated a disproportionately large amount of experience in a specific domain, making them a valuable, albeit unusual, source of knowledge.

## Key Insight
Extreme, focused practice can lead to a level of expertise that is statistically rare and represents a unique learning pathway.

## Examples
*   Ido Portal’s status as an “N of one” in the field of movement.
