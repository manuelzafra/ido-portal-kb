# Worry as the End of Love

## Definition
Worry, particularly excessive worry, represents the antithesis of love, actively undermining the capacity for connection and acceptance.

## Explanation
Drawing on the observation of Israeli doctor Nader Buto, this concept argues that a mother’s excessive worry – a desire to control and protect – ultimately diminishes her love for her child. It’s a critical distinction between genuine care and anxious preoccupation.

## Key Insight
The ability to accept imperfections and allow for vulnerability is a core component of love, while worry represents a destructive force.

## Examples
A mother constantly fretting over her child’s safety, preventing them from taking risks and experiencing the full spectrum of human experience.
