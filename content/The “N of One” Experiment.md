# The “N of One” Experiment

## Definition
The value of individual experimentation and observation in developing movement practices, recognizing that personal experience constitutes a limited but valuable dataset.

## Explanation
This concept highlights the importance of treating one's own movement practice as a scientific experiment, focusing on direct observation of internal states and responses rather than relying on external validation or established methodologies.

## Key Insight
Personal movement experience is a valid form of data, regardless of its statistical significance.

## Examples
Tracking changes in body awareness, emotional state, or physical sensations during a movement practice, without attempting to “fix” or optimize the movement.

## Relationships
* Related to: [[Scientific Inquiry]], [[Personal Exploration]]
* Opposes: [[Blind Following of Authority]]

## Notes
This echoes the scientific method – observation, hypothesis, experimentation – applied to the body.
