# 6.  Routine as a Scaffold, Not a Building

## Definition
This concept describes a routine as a temporary support structure to aid in movement, but not as a fixed, inflexible framework that limits exploration and adaptation.

## Explanation
The routine is a tool to help you get started, but it should be flexible and evolve as your understanding grows. It's a scaffolding, not the building itself.

## Key Insight
A rigid routine can stifle creativity and prevent genuine growth.

## Examples
*   “It is the scaffolding, not the building.” – Speaker 1
