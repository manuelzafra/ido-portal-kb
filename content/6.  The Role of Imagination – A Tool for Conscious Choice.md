# 6.  The Role of Imagination – A Tool for Conscious Choice

## Definition
Imagination, when employed consciously and deliberately, can be a powerful tool for shaping one’s reality and navigating uncertainty, but it becomes dangerous when used for uncritical identification.

## Explanation
The speaker distinguishes between using imagination as a tool for conscious choice and becoming lost within a fabricated reality.  It’s about maintaining awareness and agency while utilizing the power of visualization.

## Key Insight
“If you totally identify, oh, what a beautiful imagination he has. That's what we usually point at.”

## Examples
*   Guided storytelling as a method for decision-making.
