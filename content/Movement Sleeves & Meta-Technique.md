# Movement Sleeves & Meta-Technique

## Definition
A “movement sleeve” represents a dynamic, adaptable framework of movement awareness and skill, allowing for fluid transitions between different levels of control and expression. “Meta-technique” refers to the ability to operate within this sleeve.

## Explanation
This isn't a fixed set of rules or a rigid technique. Instead, it’s a flexible “sleeve” of movement potential that you can inhabit, allowing you to access and manipulate movement at different levels of detail. Operating within this sleeve – “meta-technique” – involves recognizing and adapting to the conditions of the movement, rather than rigidly adhering to a pre-determined plan.

## Key Insight
Movement mastery isn’t about perfect execution; it’s about skillful navigation within a dynamic framework of potential.

## Examples
*   A martial artist adapting their stance and technique based on the opponent’s movements, constantly adjusting their “movement sleeve.”
*   A musician improvising a solo, seamlessly transitioning between structured phrases and spontaneous, free-flowing passages.
