# <The Importance of Asking Questions of Yourself>

## Definition
A continual process of self-reflection and inquiry, challenging assumptions, examining motivations, and seeking deeper understanding of one's own behavior and experiences.

## Explanation
This involves moving beyond simply accepting information and engaging in a rigorous process of questioning one's own beliefs, values, and actions. It’s about cultivating self-awareness and using it to drive personal growth.

## Key Insight
“You don't know shit. You've heard it somewhere and someone heard it somewhere.” – Ido Portal

## Examples
*   The repeated questioning of the situation with the children – challenging assumptions about parenting and seeking a deeper understanding of the situation.
*   The questioning of one's own motivations – examining the reasons behind one's actions and choices.

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Self-Awareness]], [[Critical Thinking]]
*   Opposes: [[Blind Faith]]

## Notes
This concept underscores the importance of intellectual humility and the willingness to challenge one's own perspectives.
