# “Don’t Walk Behind Me. Walk Beside Me” - Collaborative Learning

## Definition
This phrase represents a fundamental approach to learning and teaching, emphasizing the importance of shared experience and mutual support over hierarchical instruction.

## Explanation
It’s a rejection of the traditional “guru” model of learning, where knowledge is simply transmitted from a superior to a subordinate. Instead, it advocates for a partnership – a shared journey of discovery where both parties learn and grow together.

## Key Insight
True learning occurs through collaborative exploration and shared experience, not through passive reception of information.

## Examples
*   Ido’s work with Connor – not just teaching him movements, but sharing his own process, inviting Connor to observe and learn from his mistakes.
*   The team at London Real – a collective of individuals constantly learning from each other and pushing the boundaries of movement.

*   Related to: [[Embodied Cognition]] (learning through experience)
*   Related to: [[Trust]] (the foundation of a collaborative relationship)

## Notes
This concept underscores the value of mentorship and the importance of building relationships based on mutual respect and shared learning.
