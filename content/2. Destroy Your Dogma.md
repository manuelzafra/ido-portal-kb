# 2. Destroy Your Dogma

## Definition
“Destroy your dogma” is a call to abandon preconceived notions and rigid frameworks surrounding movement, embracing a beginner’s mindset and open exploration.

## Explanation
Ido argues that many practitioners arrive with fixed ideas about what movement “should” be, hindering their ability to truly learn and grow.  The instruction is to actively dismantle these assumptions to create space for genuine discovery and adaptation.

## Key Insight
Rigid beliefs and expectations are the primary obstacle to unlocking true movement potential.

## Examples
The initial conversation with Conor McGregor illustrates this – Ido challenged his assumptions about what training and mentorship could be.
