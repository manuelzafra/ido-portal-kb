# 5. Letting Go of Control (Giving & Receiving)

## Definition
This concept highlights the need to relinquish control both when giving and when receiving, fostering a reciprocal and dynamic interaction.

## Explanation
It’s about moving beyond a rigid, prescriptive approach to movement and learning, allowing for a more fluid exchange of energy and information between the practitioner and the environment. This requires a willingness to both give and receive without judgment or expectation.

## Key Insight
“Learn to let go of control both in a position of giving and in the position of receiving it.”

## Examples
The discussion about the teacher’s role – relinquishing control over the student’s learning process and allowing the student to take ownership.

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Non-Attachment]], [[Trust]]
*   Opposes: [[Control Freak]]

## Notes
This is a core principle in Portal’s approach, emphasizing the importance of vulnerability and openness.
