# Premotor System & Permissive Movement

## Definition
The premotor system represents a layer of neural activity that anticipates and facilitates movement, opening a “gate” for motor neurons to execute movement patterns, rather than rigidly dictating them.

## Explanation
This system isn’t about pre-planning every detail of a movement. Instead, it generates a baseline level of readiness, a “potential” for movement, allowing the lower motor neurons to respond with greater fluidity and adaptability. It’s like a musician holding a note – the potential is there, ready to be expressed.

## Key Insight
Movement isn’t solely dictated by conscious intention; it’s often initiated by a pre-existing, anticipatory neural state.

## Examples
*   A tennis player’s racket instinctively moving to the ball’s trajectory before conscious thought.
*   A dancer’s body initiating a movement sequence before the conscious decision to perform it.
