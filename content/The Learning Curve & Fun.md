# The Learning Curve & Fun

## Definition
Movement training should be structured to create a learning curve that is intertwined with enjoyment and engagement, preventing over-optimization and fostering intrinsic motivation.

## Explanation
This involves intentionally introducing challenges that push boundaries, but within a framework that allows for experimentation, mistakes, and ultimately, a positive experience. The goal isn’t just to achieve a specific outcome (e.g., 80% success rate on a stick drop), but to *enjoy* the process of learning and adapting.

## Key Insight
The most effective learning occurs when the pursuit of mastery is coupled with the joy of exploration and playful experimentation.

## Examples
*   Portal’s stick drop exercise – deliberately aiming for a lower success rate to encourage adaptation and sensory awareness.
*   The diverse scenarios in the Indonesian jungle – forcing adaptation to unpredictable terrain and challenges.
