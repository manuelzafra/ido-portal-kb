# 7. Practice as a Way of Being & Becoming

## Definition
This expands the concept of “practice” beyond mere repetition to encompass a fundamental shift in one’s approach to life – a commitment to continuous learning, adaptation, and transformation.

## Explanation
It’s not just about doing exercises; it’s about cultivating a mindset of openness, curiosity, and a willingness to embrace challenges and discomfort.

## Key Insight
“Life is for learning. Life is a practice. Life is for elevation, for transformation.” – Practice is the core of a purposeful and evolving existence.

## Examples
Ido’s description of his own journey, emphasizing the importance of embracing challenges and seeking transformation.

* Opposes: [[Static Existence]]
