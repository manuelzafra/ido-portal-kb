# 2. The Practice as a Filter

## Definition
Movement practice isn't about mastering a skill; it's a method for identifying and eliminating limiting beliefs, assumptions, and ingrained patterns of behavior.

## Explanation
The act of repeatedly attempting a task, especially when facing difficulty, exposes the underlying assumptions that are holding you back.  If you can’t do something, it reveals a fundamental misunderstanding or a self-limiting belief.

## Key Insight
“It’s never applied. And I sometimes like to expose it. because nobody’s exposing it often, so I do it sometimes.” – The deliberate exposure to struggle, facilitated by practice, is the key mechanism.

## Examples
The meditation session with the advanced meditator who couldn’t perform physical tasks, highlighting the difference between theoretical knowledge and applied practice.

## Relationships
* Related to: [[Growth Mindset]], [[Deliberate Practice]]
* Opposes: [[Fixed Mindset]] (believing abilities are innate and unchangeable)

## Notes
This concept emphasizes the importance of discomfort and struggle in the learning process.
