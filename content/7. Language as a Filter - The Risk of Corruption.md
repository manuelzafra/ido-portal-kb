# 7. Language as a Filter - The Risk of Corruption

## Definition
The use of overly complex or abstract language in describing movement practices can distort understanding and hinder genuine engagement.

## Explanation
This concept highlights the potential for language to become a barrier to understanding, particularly when it’s used in a detached or theoretical manner. It suggests that the language itself can become a distraction from the core experience of movement.

## Key Insight
Clear, accessible language is essential for facilitating genuine engagement with movement practices.

## Examples
*   “It's already abused. It's already nauseating. It's already something I cringe inside to say, practice this.” – This illustrates the negative impact of using overly complex or abstract language.
*   The observation that people misunderstand the concept of “movement culture” due to the way it’s been discussed.
