# 4. The Nervous System as a Receiver & Transmitter

## Definition
The nervous system operates as a dual receiver and transmitter, constantly processing information from both the external environment and the body's internal state.

## Explanation
This frames the nervous system not just as a reactive system, but as an active participant in movement, constantly receiving and transmitting information. It highlights the importance of understanding how the nervous system is shaped by experience.

## Key Insight
The nervous system is a dynamic receiver and transmitter, constantly processing information from both internal and external sources.

## Examples
*   Moshe Feldenkrais’s concept of the body as having three elements: nervous system, mechanical system, and environment.
*   The observation that tilting the head increases alertness and influences movement.

This concept provides a foundational understanding of how the body interacts with its environment.
