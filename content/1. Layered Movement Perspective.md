# 1. Layered Movement Perspective

## Definition
A holistic approach to movement that transcends specific disciplines like sports, art, or health, recognizing that the underlying principles of movement are fundamentally more important than any particular application.

## Explanation
This concept emphasizes that movement is not simply about achieving a specific outcome (e.g., a perfect athletic performance) but about cultivating a deep understanding of the body's mechanics, sensory awareness, and the interconnectedness of movement systems. It’s about building a foundational movement intelligence that can be applied across all contexts.

## Key Insight
“Every time you spend time with Ido Quattado, you learn something.” – This highlights the transformative potential of the layered perspective, suggesting a continuous, iterative learning process.

## Examples
The entire conversation revolves around this concept – the shift from sport-specific training to a broader understanding of movement principles.

## Notes
This perspective is about recognizing that the "sum of its parts" is less than the whole. It’s a call for a more fundamental approach to movement.
