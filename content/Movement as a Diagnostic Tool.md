# 6.  Movement as a Diagnostic Tool

## Definition
Using movement itself as a method for identifying underlying imbalances, limitations, or areas needing attention, rather than relying solely on external assessments.

## Explanation
This approach involves actively exploring movement patterns to uncover the root causes of discomfort or inefficiency, rather than simply treating symptoms.

## Key Insight
Movement can be a powerful diagnostic tool, providing direct feedback about the body's state.

## Examples
*   The process of examining the wrist to determine the source of pain or limitation.

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Self-Observation]], [[Holistic Assessment]]

## Notes
This concept emphasizes the importance of embodied exploration and self-discovery in movement practice.
