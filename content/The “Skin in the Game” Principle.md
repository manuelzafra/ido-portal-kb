# The “Skin in the Game” Principle

## Definition
Maintaining a state of “skin in the game” – a sense of vulnerability and engagement – is essential for fostering genuine movement practice and unlocking deeper potential.

## Explanation
This principle suggests that a willingness to embrace discomfort, uncertainty, and even failure is crucial for cultivating a truly meaningful movement practice. It’s about being fully present and invested in the experience, rather than simply going through the motions.

## Key Insight
Genuine movement practice requires a willingness to embrace discomfort and uncertainty, fostering a state of “skin in the game.”

## Examples
Ido’s observation that his willingness to walk crowded streets in Hong Kong, despite the potential for awkward encounters, allowed him to fully immerse himself in the movement experience.

* Opposes: [[Passive Engagement]]

## Notes
This highlights the importance of cultivating a mindset of openness and willingness to experiment.
