# 3. Calculated Risk & Deconstruction of Assumptions

## Definition
A learning approach that embraces experimentation and calculated risk-taking, actively challenging ingrained assumptions and “sacred cows” through iterative testing.

## Explanation
This involves a willingness to push boundaries, even if it means experiencing discomfort or failure. It’s about systematically dismantling preconceived notions to uncover hidden truths and unlock new possibilities.

## Key Insight
True learning requires a willingness to question everything, including established knowledge and accepted practices.

## Examples
*   The child putting the spoon in their forehead – a demonstration of challenging established norms.
*   The statistical acceptance of occasional finger cuts while cutting salads.

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Scientific Method]], [[Trial and Error]]
*   Opposes: [[Dogmatism]], [[Blind Faith]]

## Notes
This concept emphasizes the importance of a critical and questioning mindset in the pursuit of knowledge.
