# 3. Contralateral and Ipsilateral Movement

## Definition
These terms describe the fundamental opposing movement patterns of the body. Ipsilateral movement involves moving the same side of the body (e.g., right arm and right leg), while contralateral movement involves moving the opposite sides (e.g., right arm and left leg).

## Explanation
Understanding these movement patterns is crucial for developing a more dynamic and adaptable movement system. It’s about recognizing how the body naturally alternates between these patterns.

## Key Insight
“Contralateral reciprocation” – A key movement pattern to be trained.

## Examples
The speaker demonstrates this through the exercises of stepping forwards and backwards, alternating feet, and the arm rotations.
