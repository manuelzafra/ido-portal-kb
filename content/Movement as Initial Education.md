# Movement as Initial Education

## Definition
Movement practices should primarily serve as a means of cultivating awareness and understanding, rather than as a tool for achieving specific physical outcomes.

## Explanation
This suggests that the initial focus of movement should be on developing a deeper understanding of the body's capabilities and limitations, with physical results emerging as a secondary consequence of this foundational awareness.

## Key Insight
The primary goal of movement should be to cultivate awareness, not to achieve a specific physical result.

## Examples
Focusing on the feeling of movement itself, rather than trying to perform a particular exercise with perfect form.

## Relationships
* Related to: [[Mindfulness]], [[Process-Oriented Learning]]
* Opposes: [[Outcome-Driven Training]]

## Notes
This emphasizes the importance of “beginner’s mind” – approaching movement with openness and curiosity.
