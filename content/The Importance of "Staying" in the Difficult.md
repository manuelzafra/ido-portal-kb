# 5. The Importance of "Staying" in the Difficult

## Definition
This concept emphasizes the value of intentionally remaining engaged with challenging or uncomfortable experiences, rather than seeking immediate escape or resolution.

## Explanation
Portal argues that true growth and transformation occur when we confront our limitations and persevere through difficulty.  The act of “staying” – resisting the urge to move on – creates an opportunity for deeper learning and self-discovery.

## Key Insight
“It’s like people make mistakes, they're not finished products. And you must be able to also see the other person and their struggles to become and actually share the journey and then chemical sexual compatibility, and then these together, boom, this is the highest chances you can have, versus all kinds of other factors which are, you know, some kind of a romantic emotional compatibility, exactly not, like opposites actually attracting that regard many times.”

## Examples
The speaker’s description of the experimental marriage, where he deliberately engaged with an incompatible partner for three months, illustrates this principle.
