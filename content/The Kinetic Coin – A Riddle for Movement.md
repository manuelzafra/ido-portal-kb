# The Kinetic Coin – A Riddle for Movement

## Definition
The “kinetic coin” is a deliberately created movement riddle – a specific, challenging movement pattern designed to stimulate neurological adaptation and exploration.

## Explanation
Developed by Ito, the kinetic coin is a playful, unpredictable movement challenge designed to force the body to adapt and explore new movement patterns. It’s a tool for unlocking potential and fostering a deeper understanding of movement. The concept is rooted in the idea of presenting “riddles” to the body to stimulate its adaptive capacity.

## Key Insight
