# 4. Aesthetics of Skill

## Definition
This describes the inherent beauty and elegance of efficient, well-executed movement, often recognized instinctively rather than through conscious analysis.

## Explanation
It’s the idea that “efficient” movement isn’t just about function; it’s about a particular *quality* that is perceived as beautiful or pleasing.  It’s a shift in how we value movement – from purely utilitarian to aesthetically driven.

## Key Insight
“That’s efficient. That’s why you don’t say efficient, you say it’s beautiful.” –  Efficiency and beauty are intertwined in skillful movement.

## Examples
The reference to the “cat-like thing” – a movement that is both graceful and economical.
