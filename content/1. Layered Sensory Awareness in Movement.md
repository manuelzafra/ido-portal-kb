# 1. Layered Sensory Awareness in Movement

## Definition
This concept centers on cultivating a deep, multi-layered awareness of the body’s sensations during movement, going beyond simply executing a technique to truly *feeling* the interaction between the body and the environment.

## Explanation
It’s about moving beyond a cognitive understanding of movement to a visceral one. It’s about noticing not just the “form” of the movement, but the subtle shifts in pressure, temperature, muscle activation, and spatial awareness that occur in real-time. This heightened sensory input allows for more precise and responsive movement.

## Key Insight
“It’s about moving beyond a cognitive understanding of movement to a visceral one.”

## Examples
The transcript repeatedly illustrates this through the “wait, wait, wait” exercise – the deliberate slowing down to become acutely aware of the initial resistance and then the subsequent release of tension.
