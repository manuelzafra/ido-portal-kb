# Constraints as Freedom

## Definition
The utilization of limitations and rules as a tool for fostering creativity, adaptability, and ultimately, greater freedom of movement.

## Explanation
This approach suggests that imposing constraints – such as restricting movement in a specific direction or limiting the use of certain body parts – can paradoxically unlock new movement possibilities and enhance overall mobility.

## Key Insight
“Like the only way to expand freedom is by limitation and boundaries.” –  Recognizing that constraints can be a powerful catalyst for movement exploration and development.

## Examples
*   Locking the knees during a squat to force the spine to stabilize and develop strength.
*   Restricting movement in a specific direction to challenge the body’s ability to adapt and find alternative pathways.
