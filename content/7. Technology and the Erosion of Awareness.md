# 7. Technology and the Erosion of Awareness

## Definition
The speaker expresses concern that the pervasive use of technology, particularly passive consumption (e.g., YouTube cat videos), is diminishing human awareness and conscious choice by prioritizing comfort over challenge.

## Explanation
The argument is that our engagement with technology is often characterized by a lack of active engagement and a tendency to seek out easily digestible content, thereby reducing our capacity for deep thought and conscious decision-making.

## Key Insight
“Am I aware or if it decreases my awareness and my conscious choices, it is by definition evil.”

## Examples
*   The comparison of passive YouTube consumption to a drain on our mental energy.
