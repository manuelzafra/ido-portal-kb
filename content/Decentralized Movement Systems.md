# Decentralized Movement Systems

## Definition
Movement practice should be approached as an open, non-hierarchical system with no fixed center or goal, allowing for engagement from any point and fostering a sense of fluidity and exploration.

## Explanation
This framework rejects the idea of a “correct” way to move, advocating instead for a system that is inherently adaptable and responsive to the individual’s experience. It’s about recognizing that movement isn’t about achieving a predetermined outcome but about the process of exploration itself.

## Key Insight
Movement is most effective when approached as a decentralized system, allowing for spontaneous engagement and exploration without the constraints of a fixed center or goal.

## Examples
Ido’s description of the “open system” of movement, emphasizing its lack of a central point and its ability to be approached from anywhere.
