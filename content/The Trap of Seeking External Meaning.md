# The Trap of Seeking External Meaning

## Definition
The tendency to outsource meaning and purpose to external sources (e.g., podcasts, books, gurus) rather than cultivating an internal sense of direction and value.

## Explanation
This concept describes the risk of passively consuming information and seeking validation from external authorities, rather than actively engaging in self-discovery and developing a personal framework for understanding the world. It’s about recognizing that true meaning comes from within, not from external sources.

## Key Insight
Don't seek meaning; *create* it through sustained, intentional action and reflection.

## Examples
*   Listening to countless podcasts without applying the insights to one's own life.
*   Following the advice of a guru without critically evaluating its relevance to one's own circumstances.
