# Boundaries as Opposing Forces

## Definition
Boundaries are not simply limitations, but rather opposing forces – love accepting back and a mother’s worry preventing that acceptance.

## Explanation
This concept highlights a crucial distinction: boundaries, when viewed solely as restrictions, are fundamentally at odds with the core of relational healing and growth. It’s a dynamic tension – love necessitates the ability to forgive and reintegrate, while excessive worry or rigid boundaries actively prevent that process.

## Key Insight
True relational progress requires balancing acceptance with healthy boundaries, recognizing them as opposing yet interdependent forces.

## Examples
A partner’s apology is accepted *because* of the boundary established around the initial mistake, not *in spite* of it.
