# 4. The Importance of “Tools” in Movement Development

## Definition
A diverse range of training tools and methods – beyond traditional strength training – that can be used to stimulate movement, challenge the body, and promote skill development.

## Explanation
This concept advocates for a holistic approach to movement training, utilizing a variety of tools and techniques to address different aspects of movement and promote adaptability.

## Key Insight
“They need sometimes tools from traditional martial arts. physiotherapy tools, movement tools, to address and improve themselves.” –  A shift from solely focusing on strength to utilizing a wider range of tools to unlock movement potential.

## Examples
*   Using tennis balls to bounce into stairs, running as the ball is released – a creative and engaging method for developing coordination and control.
*   Minimizing strength tools and maximizing the floor and walls in a movement facility.

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Versatile Movement]], [[Adaptive Training]]
*   Opposes: [[Specialized Training]] (focusing solely on a single skill or movement)

## Notes
*   This emphasizes the importance of experimentation and finding tools that resonate with the individual’s movement needs.
