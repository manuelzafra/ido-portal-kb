# Singing & Dancing as Foundational Movement Systems

## Definition
The idea that singing and dancing represent fundamental movement systems that predate and underpin more complex forms of movement, including language.

## Explanation
This suggests that the capacity for rhythmic movement, vocalization, and coordinated expression is deeply ingrained in our physiology and evolutionary history, serving as a foundational framework for all movement.

## Key Insight
Movement and communication are inextricably linked, with singing and dancing potentially representing the earliest forms of both.

## Examples
*   Hummingbirds exhibiting both dance and song behaviors.
*   The evolution of language potentially stemming from the capacity for rhythmic vocalization and movement.

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Premotor System & Permissive Movement]], [[Degrees of Freedom]]
*   Opposes: [[Central Pattern Generators as Sole Drivers]]

## Notes
This concept challenges the conventional view of language as a solely cognitive process, suggesting that it’s rooted in embodied movement and sensory experience.
