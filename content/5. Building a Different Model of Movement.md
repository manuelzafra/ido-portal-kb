# 5. Building a Different Model of Movement

## Definition
This refers to the process of consciously altering one's movement patterns, moving beyond ingrained habits and towards a more adaptable and effective approach.

## Explanation
It’s about actively constructing new movement models, rather than simply refining existing ones. This requires a willingness to challenge assumptions and experiment with new techniques.

## Key Insight
“Changing the model of how you move is more powerful than all the training you’re gonna do in your life with the same model.” –  Fundamental shifts in movement patterns yield greater results than incremental improvements within a limited framework.

## Examples
Ido’s emphasis on building a different movement model through focused practice.

* Related to: [[Neuroplasticity]], [[Learning Paradigms]]
* Opposes: [[Sticking to Familiar Patterns]]

This concept highlights the importance of deliberate practice and model-building in movement development.
