# Movement as a System of Containers and Content

## Definition
This concept frames movement practice as a system where “movements” are the containers for “content,” emphasizing the importance of selecting appropriate containers (movement forms) to effectively deliver desired outcomes.

## Explanation
It’s a metaphor for structuring a movement practice, suggesting that the specific movements chosen should be aligned with the desired learning or development goals. It’s about recognizing that movement isn’t just about doing movements, but about strategically organizing them.

## Key Insight
The effectiveness of a movement practice depends on the careful selection and arrangement of movement “containers” to deliver specific “content” – skills, sensations, or insights.

## Examples
*   Ido’s analogy of a cup of water – the container (movement) needs to be chosen to effectively deliver the desired experience (water).
