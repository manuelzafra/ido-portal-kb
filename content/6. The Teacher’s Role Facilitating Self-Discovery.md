# 6. The Teacher’s Role: Facilitating Self-Discovery

## Definition
A true teacher doesn’t *teach* movement; they create the conditions for the student to discover movement within themselves.

## Explanation
This shifts the dynamic from a hierarchical model of instruction to one of facilitation – the teacher removes obstacles and creates space for the student to find their own way.

## Key Insight
The most effective teaching is about facilitating self-discovery, not imposing knowledge.

## Examples
Sean’s class – starting with “emptying” and allowing movement to build organically.

## Relationships
* Related to: [[Socratic Method]], [[Learning Theory]]
* Opposes: [[Dogmatic Instruction]]

## Notes
This is a core principle of Portal’s teaching philosophy – it’s about empowering the student to become their own expert.
