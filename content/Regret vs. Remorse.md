# 2. Regret vs. Remorse

## Definition
Regret is a cyclical, unproductive dwelling on past mistakes, while remorse is a conscious acknowledgement of harm caused, coupled with a commitment to change and a willingness to make amends.

## Explanation
Regret is a self-inflicted wound – a constant replay of what *could* have been, fueling anxiety and inaction. Remorse, conversely, is a catalyst for growth – a recognition of the impact of our actions and a determination to avoid repeating them.

## Key Insight
The choice between regret and remorse dictates the trajectory of personal development.

## Examples
(From transcript) “Regret fuels nothing. Regret wastes energy. Remorse fuels.” – This directly contrasts the two emotional responses and their respective effects.
