# 5. Paying Attention to Internal Signals - The “Banda Mula”

## Definition
The conscious awareness and engagement of the “banda mula” – a muscle contraction in the anus – as a key indicator of stability, willpower, and efficient movement.

## Explanation
This subtle muscle contraction, often unconsciously engaged, can serve as a feedback mechanism, helping to maintain postural stability and optimize movement patterns.

## Key Insight
The banda mula is not just a reflex; it's a trainable skill that can enhance movement control and mental focus.

## Examples
*   The description of the yogi’s engagement of the banda mula to maintain stability.
