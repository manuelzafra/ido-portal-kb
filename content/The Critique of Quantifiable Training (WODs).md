# The Critique of Quantifiable Training (WODs)

## Definition
This concept argues that reducing movement to quantifiable metrics like reps, sets, and workout of the day (WODs) can be limiting and misses crucial aspects of human movement development.

## Explanation
The transcript suggests that focusing solely on quantifiable data (like CrossFit’s WODs) neglects the deeper, more complex aspects of movement – things like coordination, adaptability, and the development of a “movement intelligence.” It’s a critique of treating movement as a purely measurable quantity rather than a dynamic, sensory process.

## Key Insight
Movement should be understood and developed beyond simple quantification.

## Examples
*   The discussion of CrossFit’s WODs as an “attempt to quantify” highlights the core of this critique.
