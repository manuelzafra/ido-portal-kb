# Leaders We Deserve

## Definition
The quality of our leaders, both personally and collectively, reflects the collective choices and behaviors of the group.

## Explanation
This statement highlights the principle of reciprocal influence – we attract leaders who mirror our own values and behaviors. If a group is characterized by negativity, dysfunction, or poor choices, it’s likely to produce leaders who reflect those traits.

## Key Insight
Our leaders are a reflection of our own internal state and collective behavior.

## Examples
A community plagued by corruption and self-interest will likely produce corrupt and self-interested leaders.
