# 5. The Teacher as a Catalyst

## Definition
A “teacher” isn’t simply a source of information, but a catalyst for transformation – someone who facilitates the individual’s own journey through the labyrinth by challenging assumptions, exposing limitations, and prompting deeper self-awareness.

## Explanation
This concept moves beyond the traditional role of the teacher as a dispenser of knowledge. It emphasizes the teacher’s role in creating the conditions for transformation by pushing the student beyond their comfort zone and encouraging them to confront their own limitations.

## Key Insight
“Everybody is a teacher, and we keep switching. We all place the hat of a teacher even multiple times a day.” – Highlights the reciprocal nature of learning and the potential for everyone to be a teacher.

## Examples
The conversation about the “critical mass” and the need for a teacher to push students beyond their current limitations.
