# 2. The Limits of Language & Description

## Definition
The inherent limitations of human language to fully capture and communicate experiences, particularly those that are profoundly novel or emotionally charged.

## Explanation
The islander’s inability to describe his experience illustrates this point.  Words simply lack the capacity to convey the totality of a sensory encounter, particularly when it’s outside the individual’s existing frame of reference.

## Key Insight
“There are very few words compared to ours.” –  A recognition of the inadequacy of language to represent the richness of experience.

## Examples
The islander’s inability to describe what he saw after his helicopter trip.

## Relationships
* Related to: [[Cognitive Bias]], [[The Sapir-Whorf Hypothesis]]
* Opposes: [[Linear Thinking]]

## Notes
This highlights the importance of embodied learning – experiencing something directly rather than relying solely on verbal explanation.
