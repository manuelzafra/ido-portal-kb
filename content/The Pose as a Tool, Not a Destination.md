# The Pose as a Tool, Not a Destination

## Definition
The asana practice should be viewed as a means to an end – facilitating movement and sensory awareness – rather than an end in itself, with excessive focus on achieving a specific pose.

## Explanation
This reframes the traditional emphasis on achieving a particular posture, suggesting that the primary value lies in the movement and sensory experience generated through the practice, not the attainment of a static form.

## Key Insight
The goal is to use the asana as a vehicle for developing movement skills and heightened body awareness.

## Examples
Focusing on the feeling of lengthening in a pose, rather than rigidly maintaining a specific alignment.

## Relationships
* Related to: [[Bodyweight Movement]], [[Optimizing for Function]]
* Opposes: [[Rigid, Form-Focused Yoga]]

## Notes
This directly addresses the critique of overly prescriptive yoga instruction.
