# 7. The Pain-Reward Cycle

## Definition
Movement practice, particularly at a high level, involves intentionally embracing discomfort and pain as a necessary component of growth and transformation.

## Explanation
This concept describes the idea that pushing beyond one's perceived limits – experiencing the “ache” – is not a sign of failure but a crucial signal that growth is occurring.  It’s about recognizing that discomfort is a necessary ingredient for achieving deeper levels of movement mastery.

## Key Insight
“There is a deep sense of calmness and a deep sense of happiness, not the emotional happiness that most people refer to by knowing you are putting an effort. Together with it it's always made clear that you're not at practicing as completely and as honestly as one could.” – Highlights the paradoxical nature of experiencing pain and finding joy in the process.

## Examples
The conversation about bending the knees, the emphasis on “honesty” in movement, and the description of the “friction” that creates transformation.

## Relationships
* Related to: [[Discomfort Tolerance]], [[Growth Mindset]]
* Opposes: [[Avoidance of Discomfort]]

## Notes
This concept is about embracing the inherent challenges of movement and recognizing that discomfort is a key indicator of progress.
