# 3. Observation as a Practice – Modulating Reaction

## Definition
This concept describes a practice of cultivating non-reactive observation, initially focused on simply noticing internal states and external stimuli without judgment or immediate response.

## Explanation
The transcript suggests that a key component of transforming our relationship with experience is to develop the ability to “hold” an experience – to observe it without immediately reacting with emotion or thought. This isn’t about suppressing feelings, but about creating space between the stimulus and the response, allowing for a more considered and ultimately more constructive engagement.

## Key Insight
“If you react, you are a slave to your emotions” – the ability to observe without reacting is a fundamental freedom.

## Examples
The speaker’s reaction to a text from an ex is contrasted with the ability to remain calm and engaged after the experience.

## Notes
This practice is presented as a foundational skill for navigating the complexities of life and relationships. It’s a core element of Portal’s movement training as well.
