# The Question is More Important Than the Verdict

## Definition
This concept prioritizes the process of asking questions and exploring possibilities over arriving at definitive answers or solutions.

## Explanation
The value lies not in the final answer, but in the journey of inquiry, the critical thinking, and the continuous refinement of understanding that arises from the questioning process.

## Key Insight
Genuine progress is fueled by a relentless curiosity and a willingness to challenge assumptions.

## Examples
*   Ido Portal’s emphasis on asking questions and seeking deeper understanding, rather than simply accepting established solutions.

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Movement as Investigation]]
*   Opposes: [[Dogmatic Acceptance]]

## Notes
This concept underscores the importance of a questioning mindset in learning and growth. It’s a reminder that knowledge is not passively received but actively constructed.
