# “Bottle Popping” - A Cultural Signal

## Definition
“Bottle Popping” is a specific, established cultural signal within the London Real team, signifying a moment of significant achievement or success.

## Explanation
It’s a lighthearted, informal ritual – a celebratory gesture that acknowledges and validates hard work and accomplishment. It’s a way of recognizing and appreciating the collective effort and dedication of the team.

## Key Insight
Shared rituals and cultural signals can reinforce positive behaviors and foster a sense of community.

## Examples
*   The act of popping a champagne bottle after a successful event or accomplishment.
