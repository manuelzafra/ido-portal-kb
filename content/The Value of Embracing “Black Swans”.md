# The Value of Embracing “Black Swans”

## Definition
Recognizing that unpredictable, high-impact events (Black Swans) are inevitable and preparing for them through a mindset of openness, humility, and a willingness to adapt to the unexpected.

## Explanation
This concept stems from Nassim Nicholas Taleb’s work, emphasizing that traditional risk assessment often fails to account for rare, extreme events. It’s about accepting the inherent uncertainty of the world and developing resilience through a flexible approach.

## Key Insight
True mastery lies not in predicting the future, but in being prepared to navigate the inevitable surprises that will arise.

## Examples
*   A fighter accepting the possibility of a knockout blow during a fight and training to handle the physical and psychological impact.
*   A business leader anticipating a market disruption and developing a contingency plan to adapt to the changing landscape.

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Systemic Risk]]
*   Opposes: [[Certainty Bias]] (the tendency to overestimate the predictability of events)

## Notes
Portal uses this concept to explain why a seemingly chaotic situation like the Mayweather-MacGregor fight could still be navigated successfully. It’s about appreciating the unknown and resisting the urge to impose order where it doesn’t exist.
