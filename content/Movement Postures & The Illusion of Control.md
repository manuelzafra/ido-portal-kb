# 5.  Movement Postures & The Illusion of Control

## Definition
Many movement practices are built upon ingrained postures that, while seemingly functional, can limit true freedom and expression.

## Explanation
This concept suggests that many of our movement habits are based on learned postures that, while providing a sense of control, can actually restrict our movement potential. It’s about recognizing that these postures are not necessarily the most efficient or natural way to move.

## Key Insight
Movement postures can be limiting and should be questioned and potentially transcended.

## Examples
*   Ido’s observation that people often move in ways that reflect ingrained postures, even if those postures are not optimal.
*   The example of boxers and their reliance on linear movement patterns.

## Relationships

*   Opposes: [[Fixed Mindset]]

## Notes
This concept encourages a critical examination of movement habits and a willingness to experiment with new approaches.
