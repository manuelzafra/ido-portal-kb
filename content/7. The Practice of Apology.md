# 7. The Practice of Apology

## Definition
A genuine apology is not merely an admission of wrongdoing, but a commitment to understanding the impact of one’s actions and a willingness to make amends.

## Explanation
It’s a process of acknowledging the harm caused, taking responsibility for one’s role in it, and demonstrating a desire to repair the relationship.

## Key Insight
The ability to apologize effectively is a cornerstone of personal and relational transformation.

## Examples
(From transcript) “If you want to make somebody not back off in a fight, stand behind them with a tiny pin Literally, it can't even hurt them but it will shock them when they step backwards.” – This illustrates a practical application of the concept.
