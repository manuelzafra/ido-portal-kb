# <Living Inside the Question>

## Definition
The core concept revolves around embracing uncertainty and resisting the urge to immediately seek definitive answers. It’s a stance of perpetual inquiry, prioritizing the process of questioning over arriving at a fixed solution.

## Explanation
This isn’t about being passive or lacking direction. It’s about cultivating a mindset that welcomes ambiguity, recognizes the limitations of knowledge, and values the ongoing exploration of possibilities. It’s a recognition that the *act* of questioning is more valuable than any single answer.

## Key Insight
“The most potent thing is to create questions.” – Ido Portal

## Examples
*   The repeated questioning of assumptions about movement, learning, and life.
*   The deliberate choice to not “fix” a problem but instead to explore it through questioning.

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Socratic Method]], [[Growth Mindset]]
*   Opposes: [[Dogmatism]], [[Fixed Mindset]]

## Notes
This concept is foundational to Ido’s approach, emphasizing that true learning and transformation occur not through receiving answers, but through the sustained practice of asking “why?” and “what if?”. It’s a deliberate rejection of the pressure to have all the answers.
