# The “Doors” – Controlled Letting Go

## Definition
This concept describes a cyclical process of intentionally creating constraints (locks) to facilitate growth and adaptation, followed by strategic releases (opening doors) to embrace new possibilities.

## Explanation
It’s about recognizing that rigid adherence to plans or expectations can be limiting, while a willingness to let go of what no longer serves us can open us up to new opportunities. It’s a process of controlled experimentation, where we deliberately create conditions that challenge our assumptions and force us to adapt.

## Key Insight
“If I don't develop discipline, I cannot move beyond discipline.” – This highlights the paradoxical nature of growth – that we often need to go through a certain stage of constraint in order to move beyond it.

## Examples
*   A runner deliberately running a specific pace for a set distance, even if it’s uncomfortable, in order to build endurance and mental toughness.
*   A business owner deliberately limiting their resources or focusing on a specific niche market, in order to gain a competitive advantage.

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Multiplicity]], [[Unfolding]]
*   Opposes: [[Automaticity]] (where actions become rigid and unchangeable)

## Notes
This concept emphasizes the importance of agency and intentionality in the learning process. It’s about taking control of our own development, rather than passively waiting for things to happen.
