# The Practice as a Universal Operating System

## Definition
The core of human experience – what we call “practice” – is a fundamental operating system of the universe, mirroring natural laws across diverse fields.

## Explanation
This concept frames “practice” not as a specific skill or discipline, but as a foundational process mirroring the way the universe operates – adaptation, evolution, and the interplay of forces. It’s a holistic view where movement, learning, and even relationships are expressions of this underlying universal “practice.”

## Key Insight
Our individual journeys are reflections of a larger, universal process of adaptation and evolution.

## Examples
The principles of neurology, chemistry, and physics all demonstrate the same underlying functionality – adaptation – which is the core of this “practice.”
