# 6.  The Role of “Secrets” in Teaching

## Definition
This concept suggests that the strategic use of “secrets” – carefully curated pieces of information – can be a powerful tool for facilitating learning and transformation, rather than simply providing answers.

## Explanation
Secrets, when presented in the right context, create a sense of intrigue and encourage the learner to actively engage with the material, to seek out their own understanding, and to develop a deeper connection with the teachings.

## Key Insight
“Secrets are there to facilitate the conditions in which secrets are not needed.”

## Examples
The speaker’s discussion of the experimental marriage and the willingness to share “difficult” truths.

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Curiosity]], [[Active Learning]]
*   Opposes: [[Over-Explanation]]

## Notes
This highlights the importance of creating a dynamic learning environment that encourages exploration and discovery.
