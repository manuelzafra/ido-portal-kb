# The Dynamic Nature of Flux

## Definition
Movement is fundamentally characterized by a constant state of flux – a continuous flow of change that should be recognized and embraced as a core element of the practice.

## Explanation
This concept emphasizes that movement isn’t a static state but a dynamic process, constantly shifting and evolving. Recognizing this flux allows for a more nuanced and responsive approach to practice.

## Key Insight
Movement is inherently dynamic, characterized by constant flux and change, a quality that should be acknowledged and integrated into the practice.

## Examples
Ido’s description of the body as a “type of movement,” and his emphasis on recognizing the movement of emotions.

## Notes
This highlights the importance of cultivating a non-judgmental awareness of movement, without attempting to control or manipulate it.
