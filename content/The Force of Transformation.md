# The Force of Transformation

## Definition
The internal, often unconscious, drive to change and evolve, fueled by discomfort, curiosity, and the inherent instability of systems.

## Explanation
This concept describes the underlying force that motivates the practice – it’s not simply a conscious decision, but a powerful, often disruptive, energy that arises from the tension between the current state of being and the desire for something more.

## Key Insight
“If there was no friction inside, I wouldn't have the opportunity to choose it. That it would be mechanical again.” – This highlights the importance of embracing discomfort as a catalyst for change.

## Examples
*   A runner pushing through muscle fatigue to achieve a new personal best.
*   A student struggling with a difficult concept, ultimately leading to a deeper understanding.

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Resistance]], [[Habitual Movement]]
*   Opposes: [[Stasis]]

## Notes
This concept is a fundamental driver of the practice, emphasizing the need to actively engage with and harness this force.
