# The Practice of Disruption

## Definition
The deliberate act of challenging ingrained patterns of thought, behavior, and belief through observation, experimentation, and a willingness to embrace discomfort.

## Explanation
This concept emphasizes the importance of actively disrupting habitual ways of being – particularly those that lead to stagnation or suffering. It’s about recognizing the limitations of our existing narratives and creating space for new possibilities.

## Key Insight
“It's like what we're actually aiming for through practice is a diminished sense of ego.” –  Disruption is a key component of personal growth, requiring a willingness to question our assumptions and challenge our comfort zones.

## Examples
*   Ido Portal’s emphasis on “doing” – intentionally engaging in activities that push us beyond our perceived limitations.
*   The practice of “killing stories” – consciously letting go of limiting beliefs and narratives that no longer serve us.

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Homeostasis]], [[Equanimity]]
*   Opposes: [[Cognitive Dissonance]], [[Confirmation Bias]]

## Notes
This concept highlights the active, iterative nature of learning and growth. It’s not about passively receiving information, but about actively engaging with the world and shaping our own experience.
