# 5.  Movement as a Practice for Navigating Uncertainty

## Definition
Movement, particularly physical practices like boxing or somatic movement, is presented not as a means of achieving a specific physical outcome (e.g., a backflip), but as a training ground for developing the capacity to confront and adapt to uncertainty and instability.

## Explanation
The focus isn’t on mastering a skill, but on cultivating the mental and emotional resilience needed to handle life’s inevitable challenges. It’s about learning to “work with” the instability rather than trying to control it.

## Key Insight
“Movement is the training for life.”

## Examples
*   Learning to box – intentionally exposing oneself to physical discomfort and the possibility of being hit.
*   Employing movement practices to modulate emotions.

*   Opposes: [[Passive Acceptance]]
