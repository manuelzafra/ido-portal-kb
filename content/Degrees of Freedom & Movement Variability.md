# Degrees of Freedom & Movement Variability

## Definition
“Degrees of freedom” in movement refers to the range of possible movements that a system (e.g., a body part, a movement pattern) can achieve, and the ability to intentionally increase or decrease this variability.

## Explanation
It’s not about achieving a single, fixed movement pattern. Instead, it’s about recognizing and utilizing the inherent flexibility and potential within a movement system.  Increasing degrees of freedom allows for greater adaptability, improvisation, and ultimately, mastery.

## Key Insight
Movement isn’t about achieving a perfect, static form; it’s about embracing and skillfully manipulating the inherent variability within a movement system.

## Examples
*   A surfer adjusting their stance to accommodate changes in wave conditions, increasing their degrees of freedom to maintain balance.
*   A dancer exploring different movement qualities and rhythms, intentionally expanding their range of motion.
