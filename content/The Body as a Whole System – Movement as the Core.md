# The Body as a Whole System – Movement as the Core

## Definition
This concept emphasizes that the body is not simply a collection of separate systems (brain, muscles, etc.) but a unified, interconnected whole, with movement as the fundamental organizing principle.

## Explanation
It rejects the Cartesian dualism (mind-body separation) and proposes that movement is the primary force that integrates and animates the entire body system. It’s about recognizing that the body is fundamentally a moving system.

## Key Insight
Movement is the core process that defines and sustains the integrated functioning of the entire body system.

## Examples
*   Ido’s description of “movement” as the “magic” that ties everything together.
*   The emphasis on the “movement body-mind system.”

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Holistic Health]], [[Systems Thinking]]
*   Opposes: [[Reductionist View of the Body]]

## Notes
This concept highlights the importance of a systems-oriented approach to understanding the body and movement.
