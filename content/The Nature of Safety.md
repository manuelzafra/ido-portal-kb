# 6. The Nature of Safety

## Definition
True safety isn't a static state of protection, but a dynamic capacity to navigate uncertainty and vulnerability with resilience and self-awareness.

## Explanation
It's not about shielding ourselves from all harm, but about developing the inner resources – courage, self-trust, emotional regulation – to face challenges and learn from our mistakes.

## Key Insight
A reliance on external guarantees of safety can actually hinder growth and limit our potential.

## Examples
(From transcript) “There is no possibility to achieve safety in a system that yearns for change and transformation and everything increases in complexity and everything wants to evolve and survive.” – This frames the core paradox.

## Notes
This concept challenges conventional notions of safety and emphasizes the importance of embracing discomfort as a catalyst for growth.
