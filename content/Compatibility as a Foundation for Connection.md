# 7.  Compatibility as a Foundation for Connection

## Definition
This concept proposes that compatibility, particularly in terms of shared intentions and approaches to self-work, is a more crucial factor in successful relationships than superficial attraction or shared interests.

## Explanation
The speaker argues that a genuine connection is built on a shared commitment to growth, self-awareness, and a willingness to navigate challenges together, rather than simply seeking a harmonious blend of preferences.

## Key Insight
“If I feel like I'm a finished product and I want you to accept me but I'm not a finished product If the other person thinks, I'm not a finished product, I'm not perfect, and I'm not looking for someone to accept me as I am. I'm looking for someone to share the journey with me, of becoming.”

## Examples
The speaker’s discussion of the “unicorn” analogy and the importance of finding someone who is also “in process.”
