# Cherry-Picking as a Necessary Adaptation

## Definition
The process of selecting and prioritizing information – “cherry-picking” – is a fundamental adaptation necessary for navigating complexity and achieving individual goals.

## Explanation
Speaker 01 argues against rigidly adhering to any single “rule” or “law,” emphasizing that practitioners must selectively integrate insights based on their own experience and observation. This isn’t about abandoning principles, but about adapting them to the specific context and individual needs.

## Key Insight
Effective practice involves a dynamic, iterative process of selecting and integrating information, constantly reshaping one’s understanding.

## Examples
The practice of choosing specific training techniques or exercises based on their immediate impact and observed results, rather than blindly following a prescribed system.

## Notes
