# 4. The Samkhya Gunas & Unification of Opposites

## Definition
The ancient concept of the Gunas (qualities – Sattva, Rajas, Tamas) represents the inherent unification of opposing forces within the universe and the self.

## Explanation
This provides a philosophical framework for understanding movement and life. The Gunas aren't simply characteristics; they’re dynamic forces constantly interacting, creating the potential for new forms.

## Key Insight
Movement and life are fundamentally about the dynamic interplay and unification of opposing forces.

## Examples
Taoism’s Yin and Yang – the constant dance of complementary opposites.

* Opposes: [[Dualistic Thinking]]
