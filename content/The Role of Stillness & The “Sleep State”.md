# 5. The Role of Stillness & The “Sleep State”

## Definition
Stillness is the foundational state from which movement emerges, mirroring the “sleep state” – a state of potential before action.

## Explanation
This challenges the common assumption that movement *creates* stillness. It’s the opposite – movement arises from a state of receptive stillness.

## Key Insight
Movement is fundamentally rooted in a state of receptive stillness.

## Examples
The speaker’s observation of staring into his eyes in the mirror – a deliberate attempt to enter a state of “not knowing” to access a deeper level of awareness.

## Relationships
* Related to: [[Mindfulness]], [[Zen Buddhism]]
* Opposes: [[Constant Activity]]

## Notes
This highlights the importance of cultivating a receptive state before attempting to move or create.
