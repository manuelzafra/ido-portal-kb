# Movement as a “Game”

## Definition
Viewing movement training as a playful, exploratory process, characterized by experimentation, adaptation, and a willingness to embrace uncertainty.

## Explanation
This concept suggests that movement training shouldn't be approached as a rigid set of rules and techniques, but rather as a “game” – a space for exploration, experimentation, and playful discovery. It’s about fostering a mindset of curiosity, adaptability, and a willingness to embrace challenges and unexpected outcomes.

## Key Insight
Movement learning is most effective when approached with a playful and exploratory mindset.
