# 3. Deconcealing – The Process of Knowledge Acquisition

## Definition
This is a method of learning that prioritizes uncovering hidden potential and understanding inherent truths rather than simply acquiring information or mastering techniques.

## Explanation
It’s about stripping away layers of assumptions, preconceptions, and learned behaviors to reveal a deeper, more authentic understanding. It’s not about accumulating knowledge, but about *discovering* it through a process of active exploration and self-reflection.

## Key Insight
True learning occurs when we actively challenge our existing beliefs and allow new insights to emerge organically.

## Examples
*   “You say that in truth, secrets are everywhere. So you assume that there's already something amazing inside of us. There's already a universe of potentials inside of us. And it's the process of deconcealing these secrets that is the actual practice.”

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Socratic Method]], [[Experiential Learning]]
*   Opposes: [[Passive Learning]]

## Notes
This concept aligns with the idea of “learning by doing” and emphasizes the importance of questioning assumptions.
