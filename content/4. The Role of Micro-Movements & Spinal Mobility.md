# 4. The Role of Micro-Movements & Spinal Mobility

## Definition
The ability to perform subtle, nuanced movements of the spine and surrounding tissues is essential for maintaining long-term health and preventing chronic pain.

## Explanation
This concept emphasizes the importance of restoring the natural, fluid movement patterns of the spine, which are often lost due to inactivity or improper training techniques.

## Key Insight
Maintaining spinal mobility is a foundational element of longevity and pain prevention.

## Examples
*   Practicing spinal waves, rib cage rotations, and other movements that restore the natural movement patterns of the spine.
