# 4. The Importance of Fluctuating Movement

## Definition
This concept emphasizes that movement is inherently dynamic and fluctuating, rather than static or fixed. It’s about embracing the constant shifts in muscle activation and body position that occur during movement.

## Explanation
The body is never truly “still.” Even during seemingly stable movements, there are subtle fluctuations in muscle tension and joint angles.  Trying to force a static position is counterproductive and limits movement potential.

## Key Insight
“Movement is about the fluctuation, the differential and its control.” – The core principle.

## Examples
The speaker repeatedly emphasizes the “shake” movement, highlighting the importance of controlled fluctuations.

## Relationships
* Related to: [[Motor Control]], [[Muscle Activation]], [[Movement Variability]]
* Opposes: [[Static Alignment]] (attempting to hold a fixed posture)

## Notes
This concept is a key differentiator from traditional training methods that often focus on achieving a “perfect” or “stable” position.
