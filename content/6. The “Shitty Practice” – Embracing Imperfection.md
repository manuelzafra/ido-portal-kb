# 6. The “Shitty Practice” – Embracing Imperfection

## Definition
This concept suggests that true learning and growth occur not through striving for perfection, but through embracing and working with one’s “shitty practice” – the messy, imperfect, and often frustrating aspects of the learning process.

## Explanation
It’s about accepting that mistakes and setbacks are inevitable and that they provide valuable opportunities for learning and adaptation.  The goal isn’t to avoid imperfection, but to learn *from* it.

## Key Insight
“Because I didn't need to. Because there was nothing special about it.”

## Examples
The speaker's willingness to experiment and make mistakes, even if they lead to temporary setbacks.

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Growth Mindset]]
*   Opposes: [[Perfectionism]]

## Notes
This is a powerful antidote to the pressure to always perform perfectly.
