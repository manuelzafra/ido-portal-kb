# The “Wild and Wise” Approach to Movement

## Definition
An approach to movement that embraces both intuitive exploration and rigorous scientific understanding, combining a willingness to experiment with a deep commitment to evidence-based practices.

## Explanation
This concept, articulated by Ido Portal, suggests that the most effective movement practices are those that integrate both the wisdom of experience and the pursuit of knowledge. It’s about trusting your intuition while simultaneously seeking to understand the underlying principles of movement.

## Key Insight
True mastery of movement requires a synthesis of intuitive exploration and scientific understanding.

## Examples
*   A practitioner experimenting with unconventional movement patterns while simultaneously studying the biomechanics of those patterns.
*   A movement coach guiding a client through a challenging exercise while also explaining the physiological benefits of that exercise.

This represents a holistic philosophy of movement that emphasizes both the subjective and objective aspects of the practice.
