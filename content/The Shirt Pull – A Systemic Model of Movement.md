# The Shirt Pull – A Systemic Model of Movement

## Definition
The “shirt pull” is a training technique that highlights the interconnectedness of the entire body’s movement system, emphasizing that any change in one area will inevitably affect others.

## Explanation
This concept uses the analogy of pulling on a shirt to illustrate that all parts of a system are connected.  Any movement, no matter how small, will ripple through the entire system, affecting other areas. It’s a reminder that the body is not a collection of isolated parts but a dynamic, integrated whole.

## Key Insight
“Any system that is connected, it is one system, there are many times ripple effects of any change inside the system.” – This succinctly captures the core principle of the shirt pull model.

## Examples
*   The speaker demonstrates the ring work, manipulating the body to create specific movements and observing the resulting changes in other areas.

## Notes
