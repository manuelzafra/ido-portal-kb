# Living Inside the Question

## Definition
This concept describes a state of continuous inquiry, where movement is approached as an ongoing investigation rather than a fixed skill to be mastered.

## Explanation
It’s about remaining open to new possibilities, constantly questioning assumptions, and adapting one’s approach based on experience and feedback.

## Key Insight
True movement mastery is not about achieving a specific outcome, but about cultivating a perpetual state of curiosity and exploration.

## Examples
*   Ido Portal’s emphasis on “living inside the question” as a core principle of movement practice.

## Relationships

*   Opposes: [[Fixed Skill Set]]

## Notes
This concept highlights the importance of a growth mindset in movement. It’s a reminder that learning is a continuous process, not a destination.
