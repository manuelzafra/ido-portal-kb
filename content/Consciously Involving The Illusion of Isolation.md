# Consciously Involving: The Illusion of Isolation

## Definition
This concept describes the feeling of isolation stemming from a lack of conscious engagement with the world and others, leading to a self-limiting belief that one cannot “join” or participate.

## Explanation
It’s the paradox of feeling alone despite being surrounded by people. The feeling arises from a refusal to actively engage, a belief that one is fundamentally separate, and consequently, a limitation on one’s potential for connection and growth.

## Key Insight
Isolation is often a self-created illusion, born from a lack of conscious involvement and a belief in inherent separation.

## Examples
*   “We are all we need if we consciously involve ourselves.” – This directly states the condition for overcoming this isolation.
