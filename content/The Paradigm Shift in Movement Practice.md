# The Paradigm Shift in Movement Practice

## Definition
A fundamental change in how movement is approached, moving beyond specialized techniques and towards a broader, more exploratory and sensory-focused approach to movement.

## Explanation
This isn’t simply about learning new skills like handstands or flips. It’s about fundamentally rethinking the *why* behind movement – moving away from a goal-oriented, performance-driven mindset to one of curiosity, experimentation, and connection with the body and environment.

## Key Insight
“Be the weirdo.” – Encouraging a playful, uninhibited approach to movement, prioritizing exploration and discovery over adherence to conventional training methods.

## Examples
*   Connor McGregor’s willingness to engage in unconventional training methods like using animal movements and broomsticks.
*   The decision to “move as animals” – drawing inspiration from the natural locomotion patterns of various species.
