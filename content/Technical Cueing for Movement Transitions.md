# Technical Cueing for Movement Transitions

## Definition
The deliberate use of specific, tactile cues – often physical adjustments – to guide and refine movement transitions, particularly in complex movements like muscle-ups.

## Explanation
This involves more than just verbal instructions. It’s about providing the learner with a physical “anchor” or “reset” point to ensure they’re executing the transition correctly, minimizing wasted energy and preventing errors. It’s about creating a clear, physical roadmap for the movement.

## Key Insight
Precise technical cues, delivered through physical adjustments, are crucial for efficient and reliable movement execution.

## Examples
Speaker 00’s instruction to “touch that chest” as a checkpoint during a muscle-up, and the subsequent guidance to “lean back slightly and extend the elbows” – these are concrete, tactile cues designed to ensure the learner’s alignment and stability.
