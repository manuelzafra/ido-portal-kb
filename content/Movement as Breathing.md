# 3. Movement as Breathing

## Definition
The understanding that movement and respiration are fundamentally intertwined, with movement actively facilitating and shaping the breath, and vice versa.

## Explanation
This isn't simply about breathing *during* movement. It’s about designing movement patterns that naturally optimize the flow and quality of breath, allowing the body to move with greater ease and efficiency.

## Key Insight
Movement should *be* the breath, not just an accompaniment to it.

## Examples
*   The example of the yogi needing to adjust their movement to allow for proper breathing.
*   The observation that the strongman’s movements are inherently breath-driven.

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Pranayama]], [[Breath Control]]
*   Opposes: [[Movement as a Separate Activity]]
