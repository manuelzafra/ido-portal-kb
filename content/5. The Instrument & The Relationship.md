# 5. The Instrument & The Relationship

## Definition
The body is viewed as a “tool” or “instrument” to be utilized and understood, and the focus should be on developing a meaningful relationship with this instrument rather than simply mastering a skill.

## Explanation
Ido argues that the body is not just a physical machine to be manipulated, but a complex and dynamic instrument that deserves respect, attention, and a deep understanding. The goal is to cultivate a reciprocal relationship—one of engagement, exploration, and mutual learning.

## Key Insight
Movement practice should be centered around developing a profound and intentional relationship with the body.

## Examples
The repeated emphasis on “using it” and “changing your relationship with fear” highlights the importance of this active engagement.
