# The Practice as a Center of Gravity

## Definition
A consistent and deeply engaged practice serves as a foundational anchor, providing stability, resilience, and a framework for navigating external influences and distractions.

## Explanation
This concept suggests that a dedicated practice isn't simply a set of exercises or techniques; it’s a core element of one’s being – a source of strength, clarity, and grounding. It provides a stable reference point against which to assess and respond to the constant flux of life.

## Key Insight
A sustained practice cultivates internal stability and resilience.

## Examples
*   A daily meditation practice providing a sense of calm and focus amidst the chaos of daily life.
*   A consistent movement practice building physical and mental strength and resilience.

This emphasizes the importance of building a sustainable practice that can withstand the inevitable challenges and distractions of life.
