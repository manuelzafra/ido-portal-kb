# Conceilance - The Leap of Divergent Knowledge

## Definition
“Conceilance” is the process of combining disparate forms of knowledge – sensory experience, scientific understanding, cultural traditions – to generate novel insights and capabilities.

## Explanation
It’s not simply about accumulating information; it’s about actively synthesizing different perspectives to create something new.  Like a sculptor combining clay, metal, and wood to create a unique form, conceilance involves integrating diverse sources of knowledge to unlock new possibilities.

## Key Insight
True innovation arises from the dynamic interplay of different forms of knowledge.

## Examples
*   Combining neuroscience with traditional movement practices to develop a deeper understanding of human movement.
*   Integrating artistic expression with scientific research to explore the nature of consciousness.
