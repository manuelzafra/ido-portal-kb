# 3. The “Unbreakable” Practice - Effort as Fuel

## Definition
This concept proposes that sustained, challenging effort, particularly when approached with a specific intention, becomes a self-sustaining force, generating resilience and freedom.

## Explanation
Portal argues that simply “doing” something isn’t enough. The *quality* of the effort – the focused, deliberate, and often uncomfortable – is what truly fuels the practice and creates a sense of liberation. Suffering, when directed towards self-improvement, is not a negative, but a valuable resource.

## Key Insight
“The suffering is useful for me. The effort is useful, the right effort, not effort per se, not sadomasochistic suffering, but the ones that I use as food for working on myself, for discovering myself.”

## Examples
The speaker’s continued practice despite financial constraints and the willingness to engage in challenging experiences (like the experimental marriage) demonstrates this principle.

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Stoicism]], [[Growth Mindset]]
*   Opposes: [[Passive Acceptance]]

## Notes
This is a core tenet of Portal’s philosophy, emphasizing the active role of the practitioner in shaping their own experience.
