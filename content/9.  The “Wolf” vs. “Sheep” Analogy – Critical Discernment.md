# 9.  The “Wolf” vs. “Sheep” Analogy – Critical Discernment

## Definition
This analogy describes the need to critically evaluate external influences and advice, resisting the temptation to passively accept the opinions of others.

## Explanation
It’s about recognizing that not all information is created equal and that blindly following the advice of others can be detrimental to one’s own development.  The “wolf” must maintain its own perspective and make its own choices.

## Key Insight
“You know, people will see it from the side and they're like, what is this like with most of the things we do now people were laughing at the spinal wave now everyone is doing a spinal wave people will see this now and say like oh the portal got completely off the cliff he's doing some weird shit you know moving people so I don't know where the question is going, it's just an observation that I have.”

## Examples
The speaker’s skepticism towards popular trends and conventional wisdom.

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Independent Thinking]]
*   Opposes: [[Groupthink]]

## Notes
This emphasizes the importance of self-reliance and critical judgment.
