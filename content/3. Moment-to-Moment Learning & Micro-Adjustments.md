# 3. Moment-to-Moment Learning & Micro-Adjustments

## Definition
This concept advocates for a shift in learning from building up large, complex movements over time to making small, incremental adjustments in each moment of practice.

## Explanation
Instead of focusing on achieving a perfect, polished movement, the emphasis is on continually refining the movement based on immediate feedback. This approach acknowledges that movement is constantly changing and that the goal is not to achieve a static state but to adapt and respond in real-time.

## Key Insight
“More lifting more kilos moment to moment beautiful beautiful way to connect the things.”

## Examples
The “wait, wait, wait” exercise is a prime example – the deliberate slowing down to allow for micro-adjustments and immediate feedback.

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Deliberate Practice]], [[Feedback Loops]]
*   Opposes: [[Linear Progression]]

## Notes
This approach is about embracing the inherent instability of movement and using it as an opportunity for continuous learning.
