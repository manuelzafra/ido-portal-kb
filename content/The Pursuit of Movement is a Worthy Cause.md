# The Pursuit of Movement is a Worthy Cause

## Definition
This concept suggests that the act of continually seeking to improve movement skills, even without a specific goal in mind, is inherently valuable and beneficial.

## Explanation
The process of exploration, experimentation, and adaptation – regardless of whether it leads to a particular outcome – contributes to personal growth, increased awareness, and enhanced movement capabilities.

## Key Insight
The value lies not in achieving a specific result, but in the ongoing pursuit of movement excellence.

## Examples
*   Kelly Storet’s observation that even Usain Bolt’s post-race jog is a worthwhile pursuit.

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Movement as Investigation]]
*   Opposes: [[Goal-Oriented Performance]]

## Notes
This concept encourages a shift in focus from outcome-based training to process-oriented learning. It’s a reminder that movement is a journey of continuous discovery.
