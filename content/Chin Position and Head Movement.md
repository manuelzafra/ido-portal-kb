# 6.  Chin Position and Head Movement

## Definition
The relationship between chin position and eye movement, specifically the natural reflex of the chin going down when the eyes move upwards, and vice versa.

## Explanation
This highlights a subtle, automatic physiological response – a coordinated movement between the head and eyes – that can be leveraged to influence movement patterns and sensory input.

## Key Insight
The automatic chin-down/eye-up reflex provides a readily accessible mechanism for modulating visual input and postural control.

## Examples
*   The observation that people tend to tilt their chins downwards when looking into the distance.

*   Opposes: [[Static Posture]]

This concept demonstrates a simple, yet powerful, physiological link that can be consciously utilized.
