# 3. The Role of Playfulness & Non-Verbal Experience

## Definition
Movement practice should incorporate playfulness and non-verbal experience as entry points, fostering a deeper connection with the body and unlocking potential.

## Explanation
This emphasizes the importance of letting go of rigid expectations and embracing a playful, exploratory approach to movement. Non-verbal experience – focusing on sensation and feeling – is seen as a more direct and powerful pathway to understanding movement than verbal instruction alone.

## Key Insight
Playfulness and non-verbal experience are essential for unlocking the full potential of movement practice.

## Examples
*   Ido’s recommendation to “find the body a good entry point and then playfulness can be an entry point, an attribute or.”
*   The deliberate practice of rocking in a chair to increase movement and attention.

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Flow State]], [[Intrinsic Motivation]]
*   Opposes: [[Over-Rationalization]]

## Notes
This concept highlights the value of reducing cognitive interference and allowing the body to guide the practice.
