# Embracing Controlled Chaos in Practice

## Definition
Intentional engagement with disruption, unpredictability, and sensory overload during movement practice to foster adaptability and resilience.

## Explanation
This framework advocates for deliberately introducing elements of chaos – interruptions, unexpected movements, and external stimuli – into the practice to challenge the nervous system and build the capacity to maintain composure under pressure.

## Key Insight
Growth arises from navigating instability and uncertainty, not from seeking a state of perfect equilibrium.

## Examples
The “hanging challenge” described, where participants are subjected to interruptions and distractions during a movement sequence.
