# 5.  The “Intelligence” of the Body

## Definition
This concept suggests that the body possesses an inherent intelligence – a capacity for self-organization and adaptation – that can be accessed and utilized through mindful movement practices.

## Explanation
The body isn’t simply a collection of muscles and bones; it’s a complex, self-regulating system that strives for optimal balance and efficiency.  By paying attention to subtle cues and allowing the body to guide movement, we can tap into this innate intelligence.

## Key Insight
“The missing power in the modern culture that we're living in.” –  Highlighting the disconnect between our movement and our body's natural capabilities.

## Examples
The speaker’s emphasis on observing and responding to the body’s feedback, rather than imposing external instructions, exemplifies this concept.

## Relationships
* Related to: [[Proprioception]], [[Kinesthesia]], [[Body Awareness]]
* Opposes: [[Mechanical Training Only]] (focusing solely on external forces and resistance)

## Notes
This concept underscores the importance of a holistic approach to movement training that prioritizes the body’s inherent wisdom.
