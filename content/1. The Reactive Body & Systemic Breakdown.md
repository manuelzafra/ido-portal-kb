# 1. The Reactive Body & Systemic Breakdown

## Definition
The body’s tendency to react defensively to stress and misalignment leads to chronic damage and eventual breakdown, driven by accumulated “reactive forces.”

## Explanation
This highlights a critical understanding of the body’s response to strain – it’s not simply about immediate discomfort, but a long-term, self-destructive process when imbalances aren’t addressed. It’s a warning against forcing rigid adherence to a single, static posture or movement.

## Key Insight
Movement is about actively managing opposing forces, not passively enduring them.

## Examples
The river analogy – resisting the flow leads to being dragged down.

## Relationships
* Related to: [[Neuroplasticity]], [[Stress Response]], [[Homeostasis]]
* Opposes: [[Static Posture]]

## Notes
This concept emphasizes the importance of understanding the body's inherent reactivity and the need for dynamic, adaptable movement.
