# 2.  The “Remains” of Performance & Self-Awareness

## Definition
The recognition and acceptance of ingrained patterns of behavior and thought – “remains” of past performance – as a necessary step towards genuine change and a more honest relationship with oneself.

## Explanation
This concept acknowledges that we are all shaped by our past experiences and behaviors.  These “remains” represent ingrained patterns that, if not acknowledged and addressed, will continue to influence our actions.  The speaker advocates for observing these patterns with awareness, rather than being blindly driven by them.

## Key Insight
“It's okay to observe and be aware of that. It's okay to fuck up but be aware that you are doing certain things for the sake of something else.” –  Self-awareness is the foundation for breaking free from limiting patterns.

## Examples
The speaker’s own awareness of “manipulation” in his practice, the observation of the father judging his children, the mirror reflection.

## Relationships
* Related to: [[Cognitive Dissonance]], [[Mindfulness]]
* Opposes: [[Unconscious Behavior]]

## Notes
This concept emphasizes the importance of introspection and honesty in the pursuit of personal growth. It’s a reminder that change begins with understanding *why* we do what we do.
