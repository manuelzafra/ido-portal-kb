# The Brave New Story: A Framework for Transformation

## Definition
“The Brave New Story” is a framework for personal transformation, involving the deliberate replacement of limiting narratives with empowering ones to create a new, more fulfilling reality.

## Explanation
It’s not simply about positive thinking; it’s about a conscious, active process of rewriting one’s internal story. This involves identifying and challenging negative beliefs, and then constructing a new narrative that aligns with one’s desired future.

## Key Insight
Personal transformation is fundamentally about consciously crafting a new narrative to guide one’s actions and perceptions.

## Examples
*   “Filling that hole where the weed used to live” – This represents the active process of replacing a negative narrative with a positive one.

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Narrative Therapy]], [[Cognitive Restructuring]]
*   Opposes: [[Passive Acceptance]] (accepting a situation without attempting to change it)

## Notes
This concept is central to Portal’s approach, emphasizing the power of narrative in shaping behavior and outcomes.
