# The Role of Teachers as Catalysts

## Definition
This concept describes the role of a teacher not as a dispenser of knowledge, but as a catalyst for the student’s own discovery and evolution.

## Explanation
It’s about the teacher facilitating the student’s journey by asking questions, posing challenges, and creating space for exploration, rather than providing answers or dictating solutions.

## Key Insight
The most effective teachers are those who empower students to find their own answers.

## Examples
*   A teacher who asks a student to explain their movement choices, rather than telling them how to improve.
*   A teacher who challenges a student’s assumptions and encourages them to question their beliefs.

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Facilitation]], [[Mentorship]], [[Self-Directed Learning]]
*   Opposes: [[Authoritarianism]], [[Dogmatism]]

## Notes
This concept highlights the importance of a student-centered approach to learning, where the teacher’s role is to guide and support, rather than to control and direct.
