# The Importance of “Homo Motus”

## Definition
“Homo motus” refers to the fundamental human capacity for movement, encompassing a complex interplay of sensory awareness, neurological adaptation, and embodied intelligence.

## Explanation
This term, popularized by Ido Portal, describes the inherent ability of the human body to learn and adapt to movement patterns. It’s not just about physical strength or technique; it’s about the body’s capacity to develop a deep, intuitive understanding of movement through sensory experience and constant feedback.

## Key Insight
Human movement is fundamentally rooted in a deep, sensory-driven intelligence.

## Examples
*   Ido Portal uses “Homo motus” to describe the need to address the whole body, not just isolated muscle groups.
