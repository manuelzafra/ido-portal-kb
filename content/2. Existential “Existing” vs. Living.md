# 2. Existential “Existing” vs. Living

## Definition
This describes a state of being present without actively pursuing goals or desires, a passive existence that can be mistaken for a lack of purpose.

## Explanation
Speaker 1 uses “existing” to contrast with actively striving for outcomes. It’s a state of simply *being* in the moment, without a pre-determined intention or a desire to “make something happen.” It’s a critical distinction for understanding the purpose of practice.

## Key Insight
True living isn’t about achieving goals; it’s about the process of being fully present and engaged in the moment.

## Examples
*   “We can call it existing.” – Speaker 1
*   The contrast between passively observing and actively pursuing.
