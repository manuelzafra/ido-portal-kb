# Re-definition of Physicality

The core concept is a shift in perspective, moving beyond a purely functional understanding of the body to experience it in a more holistic and engaged way, integrating movement into daily life and challenging conventional limitations.

## Menciones

### The Power of Movement — Lewis Howes
> I want to redefine physicality and experience it in a different way. So within this chair, these constraints and within my daily life and my workstation and my relationships and my children and my dog and the way that I go to the bank, I want to redefine physicality and experience it in a different way.
