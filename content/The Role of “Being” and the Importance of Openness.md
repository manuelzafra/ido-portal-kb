# 7.  The Role of “Being” and the Importance of Openness

## Definition
The idea that movement practice should prioritize “being” – a state of openness, awareness, and acceptance – over achieving specific outcomes, recognizing the inherent variability in human experience.

## Explanation
This emphasizes that the *process* of movement – the exploration of different states and sensations – is more important than achieving a particular result. It’s about cultivating a mindset of curiosity and openness to the unexpected.

## Key Insight
The value of movement lies not just in achieving a specific skill, but in the process of exploration and the cultivation of openness and awareness.

## Examples
*   The speaker's encouragement to "play with discomfort" and explore different sensory experiences.

*   Opposes: [[Goal-Oriented Rigidity]]
