# 6. The Practitioner's Role - Beyond Instruction

## Definition
A movement teacher’s role extends beyond simply imparting knowledge or techniques; it’s about sharing their own practice and demonstrating how to *actually* practice, fostering a deeper understanding and connection.

## Explanation
This concept emphasizes the importance of the teacher’s own experience and embodiment in the learning process. The teacher isn’t just a source of information, but a living example of the practice itself.

## Key Insight
True teaching involves sharing one's own practice and demonstrating how to engage with it authentically.

## Examples
*   “You're an automatic. You go fast. This is the moment that she hasn't learned this yet.” – This highlights the importance of observing and correcting a student’s tendency to rush through a movement without fully understanding it.
*   The teacher’s deliberate slowing down of the exercise to guide the student’s attention and focus.
