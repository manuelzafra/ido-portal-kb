# 1. The Three-Month Commitment & The Gift

## Definition
A core principle emphasizing a sustained, disciplined practice (3 months) as the key to unlocking significant personal transformation, a concept often missed by those seeking immediate results.

## Explanation
This concept highlights the importance of long-term dedication to a challenging practice.  It’s not about quick wins but about cultivating a deep, ingrained habit that yields lasting benefits – a “gift” received through consistent effort.  The speaker directly addresses the common tendency to seek immediate gratification and the resulting failure to achieve deeper change.

## Key Insight
“If a person will do it like those guys here who somehow found a way to do it, they receive the gifts.” – Consistent, sustained effort is the pathway to unlocking potential and achieving meaningful results.

## Examples
The three-month practice with 30 minutes daily, referencing the workshop participants who likely abandoned the commitment.

## Relationships
* Related to: [[Habit Formation]], [[Delayed Gratification]]
* Opposes: [[Instant Gratification]]

## Notes
This concept directly addresses the common pitfalls of many self-improvement programs – the lack of sustained commitment. It’s a call to embrace a slower, more deliberate approach.
