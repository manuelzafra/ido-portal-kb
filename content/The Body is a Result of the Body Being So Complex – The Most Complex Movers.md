# The Body is a Result of the Body Being So Complex – The Most Complex Movers

## Definition
This concept posits that the human body is the most complex moving system in the animal kingdom, due to its intricate interconnectedness and ability to adapt and evolve.

## Explanation
The speaker argues that humans, through our movement patterns and neurological adaptations, have created a uniquely complex moving system. This complexity stems from the constant interplay between the body and its environment, driving continuous evolution and refinement.

## Key Insight
“And that as humans, we are the most complex movers of anything in the animal kingdom.” – This establishes the core premise of the concept – human movement is unparalleled in its complexity.

## Examples
*   The speaker’s observation of children and animals as sources of inspiration for movement patterns.

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Evolutionary Biology]], [[Neuroplasticity]], [[Movement Ecology]]
*   Opposes: [[Simplified Models of Movement]] (treating the body as a collection of isolated parts)

## Notes
*   This concept highlights the importance of appreciating the inherent complexity of the human body and its capacity for movement.
