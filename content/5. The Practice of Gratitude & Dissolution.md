# 5. The Practice of Gratitude & Dissolution

## Definition
This concept outlines a practice of intentionally directing gratitude towards those who have caused harm, aiming to dissolve rigidified emotional responses and open up space for new experiences.

## Explanation
The transcript describes a deliberate practice of choosing to feel gratitude towards those who have caused pain or harm. This isn’t about condoning their actions, but about actively disrupting the cycle of resentment and self-limiting beliefs. By consciously directing love and gratitude towards those who have caused us harm, we can begin to dissolve the emotional rigidity and open ourselves to new possibilities.

## Key Insight
“It's a practice that one has to engage with gradually, and I believe it is a more advanced phase, but you start by cultivating the love as a choice.”

## Examples
The speaker’s practice of directing gratitude towards the person who harmed him.

## Relationships
* Related to: [[Forgiveness]], [[Cognitive Restructuring]], [[Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT)]]
* Opposes: [[Resentment]]

## Notes
This is a particularly challenging but powerful practice, central to Portal’s teachings. It requires a deep level of self-awareness and courage.
