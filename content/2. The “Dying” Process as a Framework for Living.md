# 2. The “Dying” Process as a Framework for Living

## Definition
This concept reframes the human experience, suggesting that our lives are fundamentally defined by the process of “dying” – the inevitable decline and transformation that accompanies aging – rather than a linear pursuit of “living.”

## Explanation
It’s a radical perspective that challenges the Western obsession with youth and vitality. By acknowledging and accepting the reality of mortality, we can shift our focus from striving for an elusive ideal to appreciating the present moment and embracing the beauty and wisdom that come with age. It’s about finding meaning in the *process* of becoming, not just the outcome.

## Key Insight
Living authentically requires accepting the inevitability of decline and integrating it into our understanding of existence.

## Examples
*   “If this, because the process called living is not so honest, and it hints at the way out, which doesn't exist, but the process called dying informs every moment the end of the journey informs the journey. So actually we should call it dying, not living.”
