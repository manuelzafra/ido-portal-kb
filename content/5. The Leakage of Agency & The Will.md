# 5. The Leakage of Agency & The Will

## Definition
The loss of agency – the ability to consciously direct one’s actions and choices – is a significant contributor to the failure to achieve long-term goals and the development of chronic issues.

## Explanation
This concept highlights the dangers of outsourcing our decision-making and control to external forces (trends, systems, etc.), leading to a diminished sense of ownership and responsibility for our bodies.

## Key Insight
Maintaining agency – the ability to make conscious choices and direct our movements – is essential for sustained success and well-being.

## Examples
*   Following a prescribed workout program without questioning its suitability for one’s individual needs and circumstances.

This concept underscores the importance of self-awareness and critical thinking in all aspects of life.
