# Unique Postures & Language of Movement

## Definition
This concept suggests that individuals develop distinct, habitual postural patterns – both in thought and movement – that can be viewed as a “language” of movement, and that these patterns can be integrated and re-contextualized throughout life.

## Explanation
These unique postures aren't random; they represent ingrained movement habits that shape our experience. Recognizing and understanding these patterns is key to unlocking greater movement potential and adaptability.

## Key Insight
Movement patterns form a “language” that can be learned, understood, and potentially reshaped.

## Examples
*   A runner’s habitual stride pattern, a deeply ingrained movement habit.
*   A person’s habitual way of sitting, reflecting postural preferences.

## Relationships
*   Related to: [[Sensory Integration]], [[Habit Formation]]
*   Opposes: [[Rigid Technique]]

## Notes
This concept emphasizes the importance of awareness and intentionality in movement practice.
