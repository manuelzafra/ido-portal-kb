# 7. The Purpose of Practice – Beyond Technique

## Definition
The purpose of movement practice is not to achieve a specific technique or outcome, but to create a unique mode of being and becoming.

## Explanation
This rejects the idea of movement as a means to an end (e.g., a beautiful body, a specific pose). It’s about the *process* of movement itself – a continuous state of transformation.

## Key Insight
The purpose of practice is to cultivate a unique mode of being, not to achieve a fixed goal.

## Examples
The progression of purposes – from a beautiful body to living forever – highlighting the potential for corruption when a fixed purpose is adopted.

## Relationships
* Related to: [[Existentialism]], [[Process Philosophy]]
* Opposes: [[Goal-Oriented Training]]

## Notes
This concept emphasizes the importance of intrinsic motivation and a flexible approach to practice.
