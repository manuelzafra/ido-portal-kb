# The Value of “Unscripted” Movement & Trusting the Process

## Definition
This concept advocates for a movement practice that is not rigidly prescribed or controlled, but rather allows for spontaneous adaptation and exploration based on real-time sensory feedback.

## Explanation
The recording of Ido and the interviewer moving organically in London, without a script or planned movements, demonstrates the value of trusting the body’s innate ability to respond to the environment. It’s about letting go of control and allowing movement to unfold naturally, guided by sensory awareness.

## Key Insight
True movement understanding comes from unscripted exploration and trusting the body's response.
