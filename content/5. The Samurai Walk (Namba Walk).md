# 5. The Samurai Walk (Namba Walk)

## Definition
A specific walking technique originating from Japanese samurai training, characterized by a deliberate ipsilateral movement pattern.

## Explanation
This involves pushing off the right foot while simultaneously pressing the right hand against the right thigh, creating a unique gait that enhances body awareness and connection.

## Key Insight
Movement patterns can be intentionally altered to unlock new movement possibilities and improve body efficiency.
