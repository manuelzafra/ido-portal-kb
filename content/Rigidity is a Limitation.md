# Rigidity is a Limitation

## Definition
This concept highlights the detrimental effects of rigid adherence to fixed movement principles, suggesting that flexibility and adaptability are crucial for optimal movement performance.

## Explanation
Attempting to force the body into a predetermined shape or pattern can actually hinder movement efficiency and create unnecessary stress.

## Key Insight
True movement mastery requires embracing the inherent variability of the body and adapting one’s approach based on individual needs and circumstances.

## Examples
*   The critique of the “perfect squat” as a rigid dogma that can limit movement potential.
