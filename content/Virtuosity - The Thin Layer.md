# Virtuosity - The Thin Layer

## Definition
Mastery in movement isn’t about achieving a fixed level of performance but about occupying a “thin layer” of virtuosity – a state of continuous exploration, adaptation, and embracing of variability.

## Explanation
This concept challenges the traditional notion of mastery as a static state of perfection, suggesting instead that true mastery lies in the ability to continually adapt and innovate, inviting variability and chance back into the practice.

## Key Insight
True mastery in movement is characterized by a “thin layer” of virtuosity – a state of continuous exploration, adaptation, and embracing of variability.

## Examples
Ido’s description of a virtuoso as someone who “invites variability and chance back in as an opportunity to do truly new things.”
