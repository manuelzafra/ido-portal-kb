# The “What” vs. “How” of Learning

## Definition
This concept highlights the distinction between simply acquiring knowledge (“what”) and truly understanding and integrating it into one’s practice (“how”).

## Explanation
It’s about moving beyond rote memorization or simply following instructions. It’s about developing a personal understanding of the underlying principles and applying them creatively and intuitively. It’s about the *process* of learning, not just the outcome.

## Key Insight
Genuine learning involves a shift from passively receiving information to actively engaging with it and making it your own.

## Examples
*   Speaker 00’s frustration with podcasts – “Just poking a hole” – suggesting a superficial understanding of the information.
*   Speaker 00’s focus on “doing” rather than “knowing” – emphasizing the importance of practical application.
