# 3. Ground Reaction Force & Vertical Movement

## Definition
The force exerted by the ground upwards can be harnessed to generate vertical movement, creating an “elevator” effect.

## Explanation
This demonstrates a sophisticated understanding of biomechanics. It’s not just about gravity pulling you down; it’s about recognizing and utilizing the ground’s response to your downward movement.

## Key Insight
Movement can be amplified by intelligently utilizing the ground’s reactive force.

## Examples
The example of the “crazy vertical jumps” of kids with skinny legs – they’re effectively using ground reaction force to maximize their jump height.
