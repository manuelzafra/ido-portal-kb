# 7.  The Illusion of a Life & The Practice as a Mode of Becoming

## Definition
This concept describes the realization that our everyday lives are often a constructed illusion, and that true meaning and purpose are found in the practice itself, which is a continuous process of becoming.

## Explanation
Speaker 01 suggests that we often mistake our daily routines and achievements for a “life,” but that this is an illusion. The practice, in contrast, is a dynamic mode of becoming, constantly evolving and shaping our understanding of ourselves and the world.

## Key Insight
The practice is not merely a means to an end (e.g., a perfect posture), but a continuous process of transformation and growth.

## Examples
*   “Why did we even begin this practice? Why did we even start?” – Speaker 01
*   The idea that the practice is a “mode of becoming.”
